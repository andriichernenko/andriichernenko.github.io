        1. Sights & walks in Barcelona (sorted by travel time from the hotel)

        1.1 Gothic Quarter & Barcelona Cathedral — old town on foot (open all year) 🗺

        What: The Barri Gòtic: Roman walls, the Cathedral (rooftop terrace extra), Plaça Sant Jaume (city hall), narrow medieval streets. Best early morning (before 10:00) or after 19:00 to avoid tour groups and cruise crowds.

        Getting there: 5 min walk from the hotel (Carrer de Jaume I → Plaça de la Seu).

        Winter caveats: free, always open outdoors; attraction interiors vary — see §1.3, §1.20. On 31 Dec the lower Rambla/Port Vell area has race-related street closures ~16:00–20:00 — stay on the Gothic/El Born side or use the metro.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Plaça de la Seu / Cathedral

        Source: barcelona.cat — Barri Gòtic

        1.2 Santa Maria del Mar & El Born (open all year) 🗺

        What: The 14th-century basilica of Santa Maria del Mar (free, donations welcome) and the El Born lane maze — Passeig del Born, Carrer de la Princesa, Carrer de Montcada. Quieter than the Gothic core.

        Getting there: 6 min walk from the hotel.

        Winter caveats: church open daily (check mass times); streets free and always open.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Santa Maria del Mar, Plaça de Santa Maria

        Source: santamariadelmarbcn.org

        1.3 Palau de la Música Catalana — Modernist concert hall (open all year; concerts book ahead) 🗺

        What: Domènech i Montaner's 1908 concert hall, UNESCO-listed. Guided day visits plus the New Year concert series 28 Dec–9 Jan (see holiday table). Book concert tickets ahead; day-visit slots also sell out around holidays.

        Getting there: 9 min walk from the hotel (Carrer del Palau de la Música 4).

        Winter caveats: open daily; 1 Jan has the 21:00 New Year concert — normal visits may be limited that day, verify.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — entrance, Carrer del Palau de la Música 4

        Source: palaumusica.cat — New Year concert, palaumusica.cat

        1.4 Port Vell — Moll de la Fusta & Nadal al Port (seasonal: 27 Nov 2026 – 6 Jan 2027) 🗺

        What: The Christmas-at-the-port event: market stalls, 30 m lit tree, floating nativity in the harbour, ice rink and activities, open daily 11:00–23:00. Evening lights are the best time; combine with a Barceloneta beach walk (§1.5).

        Getting there: 11 min walk from the hotel along Via Laietana to the waterfront.

        Winter caveats: seasonal event — closes 6 Jan 2027; on 31 Dec the race route passes nearby (Passeig de Picasso) ~16:00–20:00, use the metro or come after 20:00.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Moll de la Fusta

        Source: nadalalportvell.com, Port de Barcelona — Nadal al Port, Museu Marítim — Fira de Nadal del Port 2026

        1.5 Barceloneta beach & old port (open all year) 🗺

        What: The city beach and fishermen's quarter; pleasant midday winter walks (sea ~14 °C — walking only). Good escape route from the crowded centre.

        Getting there: 9 min walk from the hotel (Via Laietana → Pg. de Colom).

        Winter caveats: free, always open; windy in winter — bring a jacket.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Platja de la Barceloneta

        Source: barcelona.cat — Barceloneta

        1.6 Casa Batlló — Gaudí on Passeig de Gràcia (open daily; book ahead) 🗺

        What: Gaudí's 1904 apartment block — façade, Noble Floor, rooftop chimneys, immersive light show. Winter is low season but the Gaudí centenary keeps demand high; book online and take the earliest slot.

        Getting there: 10 min on L4 from Jaume I (Passeig de Gràcia station).

        Winter caveats: open every day of the year; winter general visit ~9:00–18:30 (evening "Magic Nights" slots to 22:30 exist — check product). Book ahead.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Pg. de Gràcia 43

        Source: casabatllo.es — tickets

        1.7 Passeig de Gràcia façades — free Gaudí/Modernist street walk (open all year) 🗺

        What: Free outdoor walk down Passeig de Gràcia: Casa Batlló (§1.6), Casa Amatller (chocolate façade), Casa Lleó Morera, and La Pedrera (§1.8). The buildings' exteriors are the attraction — no ticket needed.

        Getting there: 10–12 min on L4 from Jaume I (Passeig de Gràcia).

        Winter caveats: outdoors, always accessible; best light 15:00–17:00 before dark.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Passeig de Gràcia

        Source: barcelona.cat — Passeig de Gràcia

        1.8 La Pedrera (Casa Milà) (open daily; book ahead) 🗺

        What: Gaudí's last civil work — rooftop with warrior chimneys, attic, apartment. In the window 26 Dec–3 Jan the day visit runs 9:00–18:30 (last entry 17:30).

        Getting there: 12 min on L4/L3 from Jaume I (Passeig de Gràcia / Diagonal).

        Winter caveats: Night Experience closed 24, 25 and 31 Dec; 1 Jan day visit likely normal winter hours — verify on site.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Pg. de Gràcia 92

        Source: lapedrera.com

        1.9 Sagrada Família — ★ book timed ticket NOW (open daily; 1 Jan 9:00–14:00) 🗺

        What: Gaudí's unfinished basilica, Barcelona's icon. Timed-entry tickets €26 (€36 with tower); 2026 Gaudí centenary = exceptionally high demand, slots sell out days ahead. Tue–Thu are statistically the quietest days; take the first slot (9:00).

        Getting there: 16 min on L4 from Jaume I (Sagrada Família station); 26 min walk.

        Winter caveats: Mon–Sat 9:00–18:00, Sun 10:30–18:00; 1 Jan 9:00–14:00. Last entry 1 h before closing. Sunday international mass in the morning.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Carrer de Mallorca 401

        Source: sagradafamilia.org — hours, sagradafamilia.org — prices

        1.10 Recinte Modernista de Sant Pau (open; likely closed 1 Jan) 🗺

        What: Domènech i Montaner's 1916 hospital complex, UNESCO-listed, 10 min walk from the Sagrada Família — far quieter, equally stunning (tunnels, pavilions, gardens).

        Getting there: 22 min on L4 + bus from Jaume I; or walk from Sagrada Família (~10 min).

        Winter caveats: Nov–Mar Mon–Sat 9:30–17:00, Sun & holidays 9:30–15:00. Municipal site — likely closed 1 Jan; verify before going. Book online.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Carrer de Sant Antoni Maria Claret 167

        Source: santpaubarcelona.org, irBarcelona — Sant Pau hours

        1.11 Park Güell — ★ book 9:30 slot NOW (open every day incl. 1 Jan) 🗺

        What: Gaudí's park: the Monumental Zone (ticketed, €18) with the mosaic terrace, benches and gatehouses; the rest of the park is free. Strict 30-min entry windows — no grace period. Winter light is best in the morning.

        Getting there: 29 min from Jaume I (L4 + bus V19/116 to the top entrances, or L3 Lesseps + uphill walk).

        Winter caveats: open 365 days; Monumental Zone opens 9:30, winter last entry ~16:30–17:30 (exact per date on the official calendar). Gaudí House Museum inside the park is temporarily closed. May close in extreme weather — check the disruptions page.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Monumental Zone entrance

        Source: parkguell.barcelona — prices & times, parkguell.barcelona — disruptions

        What: MACBA (contemporary art, strong collection) and CCCB next door; the Plaça dels Àngels skateboard square between them. El Raval is lively and local in the evenings.

        Getting there: 15 min walk from the hotel into El Raval.

        Winter caveats: MACBA Mon & Wed–Fri 11:00–19:30, Sat 10:00–20:00, Sun 10:00–15:00, closed Tue and 1 Jan; expansion works at Plaça dels Àngels until Feb 2027 — follow the signs to the temporary entrance. Free first Sunday (1st of month) and Sat from 18:00 in summer hours (winter free slots are first Sunday only, with reservation).

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Plaça dels Àngels 1

        1.13 Museu Picasso — El Born (closed Mon & 1 Jan; 31 Dec 10:00–14:00) 🗺

        What: World's largest Picasso collection by count, focused on his formative years, in five medieval palaces on Carrer de Montcada.

        Getting there: 15 min walk from the hotel (El Born).

        Winter caveats: Tue–Sun 10:00–19:00; closed Mon, 1 Jan, 25 Dec; 31 Dec 10:00–14:00. Free Thu 16:00–19:00 and first Sunday of month (Sun 3 Jan) — reservation released 4 days ahead at 10:00 (29 Dec 10:00), and those slots are the most crowded.         1.14 MNAC — national art museum, Palau Nacional on Montjuïc (closed Mon & 1 Jan) 🗺

        What: Catalan art from Romanesque frescoes to Gaudí/modernisme, in the Palau Nacional above the Magic Fountain. Great city views from the terrace.

        Getting there: ~30 min: L3 from Liceu (3 min walk from hotel) to Paral·lel/Plaça Espanya, then bus 150/55 or walk up, or L1 to Espanya + escalators.

        Winter caveats: Tue–Sat 10:00–18:00, Sun 10:00–15:00, closed Mon and 1 Jan. Free Sat from 15:00 (online booking) and first Sunday.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Palau Nacional, Parc de Montjuïc

        1.15 Fundació Joan Miró — on Montjuïc (closed Mon & 1 Jan) 🗺

        What: Miró's complete world — 10,000+ works, sculpture garden, terrace views over the city.

        Getting there: ~30 min, same as MNAC (§1.14), then a 10 min walk through the gardens.

        Winter caveats: Tue–Sun 10:00–19:00 winter, closed Mon and 1 Jan. Free first Sunday with reservation.

        1.16 Castell de Montjuïc & Cementiri de Montjuïc (open all year) 🗺

        What: The 17th-century fortress at the top of Montjuïc (city views, military museum) and the serene modernist cemetery with graves of Catalan figures — free, open daily 8:00–18:00 (cemetery).

        Getting there: ~35 min: L3 to Espanya, then the Montjuïc funicular (winter: Mon–Fri 7:30–20:00, weekends/holidays 9:00–20:00) or bus 150 to the castle.

        Winter caveats: cemetery daily 8:00–18:00 (closes earlier in winter — check); castle open daily, check 1 Jan hours. Combine with MNAC (§1.14).

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Castell de Montjuïc

        Source: barcelona.cat — Castell de Montjuïc, cementirimontjuic.barcelona

        1.17 Bunkers del Carmel (Turó de la Rovira) — 360° viewpoint (open all year, always free) 🗺

        2. Day trips by public transport (sorted by travel time; no car)

        All trains leave from Barcelona-Sants (18 min from the hotel: L3 from Liceu, or L4→L5 from Jaume I) or Plaça d'Espanya / Plaça de Catalunya (FGC).         2.1 Girona — medieval old town (~40 min by AVE) 🗺

        What: One of Catalonia's most beautiful old towns: the Onyar river colourful houses (postcard view), the 15th-century cathedral with the world's widest Gothic nave, Arab baths, Jewish quarter. Compact — 3–4 h is plenty.

        Getting there: AVE/Avant from Sants ~38–40 min (book on Renfe for best prices; ~€10–20), or Rodalies R11 ~1 h 25 min (no booking, ~€9). Girona station is an 8 min walk from the old town. From the hotel: 18 min to Sants + train.

        Winter caveats: cathedral Tue–Sat 10:00–16:30, Sun 12:00–16:30; old town streets always open. 1 Jan: holiday train timetable, fewer trains.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Girona old town / cathedral

        Source: girona.cat, renfe.com

        2.2 Penedès cava country — Sant Sadurní d'Anoia & Vilafranca (~50 min by R4) 🗺

        What: The capital of cava: Freixenet's cellar is right at the station; Codorníu's Modernist palace (Puig i Cadafalch) is a 1.5 km walk; VINSEUM wine museum in Vilafranca del Penedès. Winter = quiet, tastings by appointment. Book winery visits in advance — they cap capacity.

        Getting there: Rodalies R4 from Plaça de Catalunya / Arc de Triomf / Sants to Sant Sadurní d'Anoia ~45–50 min (~€6.90), then Vilafranca ~10 min more. From the hotel: 18 min to Sants (L3) + R4.

        Winter caveats: Freixenet tours Mon–Sun 10:00–15:00 (Sat to 17:00) per Maps — verify slot when booking; VINSEUM Tue–Sat 10:00–19:00, Sun 10:00–14:00, closed Mon. 1 Jan: reduced trains, wineries likely closed.
