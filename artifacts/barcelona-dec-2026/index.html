          1. Sights & walks in Barcelona (sorted by travel time from the hotel)

          1.1 Gothic Quarter & Barcelona Cathedral — old town on foot (open all year) 🗺

          What: The Barri Gòtic: Roman walls, the Cathedral (rooftop terrace extra), Plaça Sant Jaume (city hall), narrow medieval streets. Best early morning (before 10:00) or after 19:00 to avoid tour groups and cruise crowds.

          Getting there: 5 min walk from the hotel (Carrer de Jaume I → Plaça de la Seu).

          Winter caveats: free, always open outdoors; attraction interiors vary — see §1.3, §1.20. On 31 Dec the lower Rambla/Port Vell area has race-related street closures ~16:00–20:00 — stay on the Gothic/El Born side or use the metro.

          Links: Google Maps · geo: — Plaça de la Seu / Cathedral

          Source: barcelona.cat — Barri Gòtic

          1.2 Santa Maria del Mar & El Born (open all year) 🗺

          What: The 14th-century basilica of Santa Maria del Mar (free, donations welcome) and the El Born lane maze — Passeig del Born, Carrer de la Princesa, Carrer de Montcada. Quieter than the Gothic core.

          Getting there: 6 min walk from the hotel.

          Winter caveats: church open daily (check mass times); streets free and always open.

          Links: Google Maps · geo: — Santa Maria del Mar, Plaça de Santa Maria

          Source: santamariadelmarbcn.org

          1.3 Palau de la Música Catalana — Modernist concert hall (★ concerts book ahead — see table above) 🗺

          What: Domènech i Montaner's 1908 concert hall, UNESCO-listed. Guided day visits plus the New Year concert series 28 Dec–9 Jan (see holiday table). Book concert tickets ahead; day-visit slots also sell out around holidays.

          Getting there: 9 min walk from the hotel (Carrer del Palau de la Música 4).

          Winter caveats: open daily; 1 Jan has the 21:00 New Year concert — normal visits may be limited that day, verify.

          Links: Google Maps · geo: — entrance, Carrer del Palau de la Música 4

          Source: palaumusica.cat — New Year concert, palaumusica.cat

          1.4 Port Vell — Moll de la Fusta & Nadal al Port (seasonal: 27 Nov 2026 – 6 Jan 2027) 🗺

          What: The Christmas-at-the-port event: market stalls, 30 m lit tree, floating nativity in the harbour, ice rink and activities, open daily 11:00–23:00. Evening lights are the best time; combine with a Barceloneta beach walk (§1.5).

          Getting there: 11 min walk from the hotel along Via Laietana to the waterfront.

          Winter caveats: seasonal event — closes 6 Jan 2027; on 31 Dec the race route passes nearby (Passeig de Picasso) ~16:00–20:00, use the metro or come after 20:00.

          Links: Google Maps · geo: — Moll de la Fusta

          Source: nadalalportvell.com, Port de Barcelona — Nadal al Port, Museu Marítim — Fira de Nadal del Port 2026
