Two travellers, first visit, based at **Hotel Gótico, Carrer de Jaume I 14, Ciutat Vella** (check-in 15:00 / check-out 12:00). No car — everything on foot, metro, FGC and Rodalies trains. Crowd-averse: first-slot tickets on weekdays, and New Year's Eve kept local instead of the Plaça Espanya mega-crowd. Food and drink are local bars, markets and bodegas — no international chains. - **Thu 31 Dec (Nochevieja, not a holiday):** metro runs **all night**; free fireworks/drone show at Av. Reina Maria Cristina / Magic Fountain, Plaça d'Espanya ~23:50–00:10 — **100,000+ people**, avoid if you want no crowds (it is 19 min from the hotel on L3). Cursa dels Nassos 10 km race: streets closed ~16:00–20:00 in Poblenou and along Passeig de Picasso/Port Vell (start 17:15, Carrer de Selva de Mar). Most restaurants do fixed-price *menú de Cap d'Any* dinners, booked solid weeks ahead — book now or eat an early lunch. Picasso Museum open 10:00–14:00 only on 31 Dec.

Stock up on food on 31 Dec.
- **Sat 2 Jan:** normal Saturday; metro runs all night into Sun. Tibidabo amusement park open 11:00–18:00 (Jan 2–5 only in deep winter).
- **Sun 3 Jan:** markets closed (all municipal markets close Sundays); many small restaurants closed Sundays; **first Sunday of the month** = free entry at Picasso/MNAC/MACBA but only with an advance reservation released 4 days ahead at 10:00 (i.e. **Tue 29 Dec 10:00**) — and it is the most crowded museum day. MNAC Sun 10:00–15:00.

## 1. Sights & walks in Barcelona (sorted by travel time from the hotel)

### 1.1 Gothic Quarter & Barcelona Cathedral — old town on foot (**open all year**)
**What:** The Barri Gòtic: Roman walls, the Cathedral (rooftop terrace extra), Plaça Sant Jaume (city hall), narrow medieval streets. Best early morning (before 10:00) or after 19:00 to avoid tour groups and cruise crowds.

**Getting there:** 5 min walk from the hotel (Carrer de Jaume I → Plaça de la Seu).

**Winter caveats:** free, always open outdoors; attraction interiors vary — see §1.3, §1.20. On 31 Dec the lower Rambla/Port Vell area has race-related street closures ~16:00–20:00 — stay on the Gothic/El Born side or use the metro.

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=41.3828639,2.1767069) · [geo:](geo:41.3828639,2.1767069) — Plaça de la Seu / Cathedral

**Source:** [barcelona.cat — Barri Gòtic](https://www.barcelona.cat/en/que-hacer-en-bcn/ciudad-vieja)

### 1.2 Santa Maria del Mar & El Born (**open all year**)
**What:** The 14th-century basilica of Santa Maria del Mar (free, donations welcome) and the El Born lane maze — Passeig del Born, Carrer de la Princesa, Carrer de Montcada. Quieter than the Gothic core.

**Getting there:** 6 min walk from the hotel.

**Winter caveats:** church open daily (check mass times); streets free and always open.

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=41.38361,2.17649) · [geo:](geo:41.38361,2.17649) — Santa Maria del Mar, Plaça de Santa Maria

## 2. Day trips by public transport (sorted by travel time; no car)

All trains leave from **Barcelona-Sants** (18 min from the hotel: L3 from Liceu, or L4→L5 from Jaume I) or **Plaça d'Espanya / Plaça de Catalunya (FGC)**. On **1 Jan** FGC and Rodalies run reduced holiday timetables — prefer day trips on 30/31 Dec or 2 Jan. Buy Rodalies tickets at machines (no reservation); Sitges needs a Zone 2 ticket.

### 2.1 Girona — medieval old town (~40 min by AVE)
**What:** One of Catalonia's most beautiful old towns: the Onyar river colourful houses (postcard view), the 15th-century cathedral with the world's widest Gothic nave, Arab baths, Jewish quarter. Compact — 3–4 h is plenty.

**Getting there:** AVE/Avant from Sants ~38–40 min (book on Renfe for best prices; ~€10–20), or Rodalies R11 ~1 h 25 min (no booking, ~€9). Girona station is an 8 min walk from the old town. From the hotel: 18 min to Sants + train.

**Winter caveats:** cathedral Tue–Sat 10:00–16:30, Sun 12:00–16:30; old town streets always open. 1 Jan: holiday train timetable, fewer trains.

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=41.9874123,2.8258715) · [geo:](geo:41.9874123,2.8258715) — Girona old town / cathedral

**Source:** [girona.cat](https://www.girona.cat/), [renfe.com](https://www.renfe.com/)

### 2.2 Penedès cava country — Sant Sadurní d'Anoia & Vilafranca (~50 min by R4)
**What:** The capital of cava: Freixenet's cellar is right at the station; Codorníu's Modernist palace (Puig i Cadafalch) is a 1.5 km walk; VINSEUM wine museum in Vilafranca del Penedès. Winter = quiet, tastings by appointment. **Book winery visits in advance** — they cap capacity.

**Getting there:** Rodalies R4 from Plaça de Catalunya / Arc de Triomf / Sants to Sant Sadurní d'Anoia ~45–50 min (~€6.90), then Vilafranca ~10 min more. From the hotel: 18 min to Sants (L3) + R4.

**Winter caveats:** Freixenet tours Mon–Sun 10:00–15:00 (Sat to 17:00) per Maps — verify slot when booking; VINSEUM Tue–Sat 10:00–19:00, Sun 10:00–14:00, closed Mon. 1 Jan: reduced trains, wineries likely closed.

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=41.4205156,1.7946846) · [geo:](geo:41.4205156,1.7946846) — Sant Sadurní station; cava cellars: [Codorníu](https://www.google.com/maps?q=41.4336955,1.7968728) [Freixenet](https://www.google.com/maps?q=41.4203698,1.7936461) [VINSEUM](https://www.google.com/maps?q=41.3466605,1.6969049)

**Source:** [freixenet.com — visits](https://freixenet.com/es/en/visit/freixenet-tour/), [codorniu.com — visits](https://www.codorniu.com/), [vinseum.cat](https://www.vinseum.cat/), [Barcelona Urbana — Penedès day trip guide](https://barcelonaurbana.com/en/blog/penedes-day-trip-from-barcelona)

## Itinerary — six days, one base

Each day: one main destination; return to the hotel base in the evening. Times are from Hotel Gótico, Carrer de Jaume I 14.

### I1. Tue 29 Dec — arrive & settle: Gothic Quarter, El Born, Port Vell (§1.1, §1.2, §1.4, §5.4) [View on map](map.html?i=I1)
Check in from 15:00. Late afternoon walk: Gothic Quarter + Santa Maria del Mar (early-evening quiet), then down Via Laietana to **Nadal al Port Vell** (§1.4) for the lights. Dinner: **El Xampanyet** (§5.4, 6 min walk — cava & tapas, open 19:00–23:00). Overnight at base.

### I2. Wed 30 Dec — Gaudí day: Sagrada Família 9:00 + Sant Pau + Park Güell (§1.9, §1.10, §1.11) [View on map](map.html?i=I2)
Leave 8:35, L4 from Jaume I (16 min) for the **first Sagrada Família slot 9:00** (§1.9, booked). Walk 10 min to **Sant Pau** (§1.10, 22 min by transit). Afternoon: **Park Güell** — book a late-morning/early-afternoon slot to catch winter light (§1.11). Sunset 17:20 — Bunkers del Carmel (§1.17, 38 min) if energy allows. Dinner: **Bar Cañete** (§5.1, 9 min walk, booked). Overnight at base.

### I3. Thu 31 Dec — Montserrat day trip + quiet local NYE (§2.4) [View on map](map.html?i=I3)
L3 from Liceu to Plaça d'Espanya (19 min), FGC R5 + Cremallera/Aeri (~1 h 25 min total) — **weekday = quietest day**. Escolania choir 13:00. Monastery entry €18 online. Back in Barcelona by ~17:00; race street closures ~16:00–20:00 near Poblenou/Port Vell — stay in Ciutat Vella. Early dinner at a booked spot or hotel; **do not go to Plaça Espanya/Magic Fountain** (100k crowd) — celebrate NYE on your own street: 12 grapes + cava at midnight (§5.16). Metro runs all night. Overnight at base.

### I4. Fri 1 Jan — holiday-quiet day: Sagrada Família or Park Güell + beach & Nadal al Port (§1.9, §1.11, §1.4, §1.5) [View on map](map.html?i=I4)
Shops/most restaurants/museums closed — eat at the hotel or a confirmed-open tourist-zone spot (stocked up 31 Dec). Morning: **Sagrada Família 9:00–14:00** (§1.9) or **Park Güell** (§1.11, open normally). Afternoon: Barceloneta beach walk (§1.5) + **Nadal al Port Vell** (§1.4, open 11:00–23:00). Optional: Palau de la Música **New Year concert 21:00** (§1.3, booked). Metro 5:00–24:00. Overnight at base.

### I5. Sat 2 Jan — day trip: Sitges or Penedès cava (§2.3 or §2.2, plus §1.18 optionally) [View on map](map.html?i=I5)
Option A: L4 to Passeig de Gràcia + R2 Sud to **Sitges** (~50 min train) — old town, Cau Ferrat museum (open Sat 10:00–19:00), beach walk (§2.3). Option B: Sants + R4 to **Sant Sadurní** for a booked cava cellar visit (Freixenet at the station / Codorníu), lunch in town, VINSEUM in Vilafranca if time (§2.2). Metro all night tonight. Dinner back in Barcelona: **XERA** (§4.1, opens 18:30) or **Bar Calders** (§4.2, opens 11:00 Sat). Overnight at base.

### I6. Sun 3 Jan — slow morning, last sights, departure (§1.12, §1.13, §5.7) [View on map](map.html?i=I6)
Check out by 12:00 — ask the hotel to store luggage. Early Gothic Quarter/El Born (emptiest Sunday morning), **MACBA** (§1.12, Sun 10:00–15:00) or **Museu Picasso** (§1.13, Sun 10:00–19:00; first-Sunday free needs the 29 Dec 10:00 reservation and is crowded — just buy a ticket). Sunday vermut at **Bodega del Vermut** Gràcia (§5.7, 12:00–16:00). Markets closed — Sunday breakfast at **Forns del Pi** (§3.1, open Sun 7:00–21:00) or **La Pallaresa** (§3.15, open Sun 9:00–13:00). Afternoon: departure (L9 airport ticket €5.90, or L3 to Paral·lel + Aerobus).
