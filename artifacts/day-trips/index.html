      1. Hikes & nature

      1.1 Paradiset nature reserve — forest, lakes and a historic torp

      What: Paradiset-Hanveden, a large forest reserve in Huddinge's southern tip: old-growth patches, the lakes Trekanten and Trehörningen with bathing rocks, several marked trails (Sörmlandsleden passes through), six fireplaces and the old torp Paradiset with a raststuga.

      Drive: ~30–40 min, ~25–30 km via väg 259 (Haningeleden), exit toward "Ådran / Ekedal / Paradiset", then Ådravägen ~1.6 km to the main parking.

      November caveats: the reserve is open every day, all year (verified). The torp's raststuga is closed on Sundays in winter (reopens in March), so bring your own fika; fire only at the designated fireplaces (bring your own wood). Short daylight — headlamp recommended.

      Links: Google Maps · geo: — Entré Paradiset main parking

      Source: naturkartan.se — Entré Paradiset, lansstyrelsen.se — Paradiset, paradiset-hanveden.se

      1.2 Femöre peninsula — coastal cliffs, and a closed Cold War fort

      What: 235 ha reserve on the peninsula south of Oxelösund: cliffs, sea-buckthorn, coastal pine forest; the Sörmlandsleden "Femöre runt" loop goes around it. Under Femörehuvud sits Femörefortet, an intact 1966–2003 coastal artillery battery blasted into the rock.

      Drive: ~1 h 20–30 min, ~120–125 km via E4 to Nyköping then väg 53 to Oxelösund, over Femörekanalen.

      November caveats: the reserve is open year-round (verified), but the fort's last public 2026 tours ended 30 October — in November the fort interior is closed (group bookings only). Winter birding is good (sea eagles, eiders in the ice-free channel). Exposed cliffs: wind, spray ice, slippery rocks; never trust the ice in Femörekanalen. Fire only at designated grillplatser.

      Links: Google Maps · geo: — Femörefortets parkering (stora parkeringen; also fiskehamn parking: Maps · geo:)

      Source: lansstyrelsen.se — Femöre, femorefortet.se, visitoxelosund.se

      1.3 Nynäs — manor park and coastal trails (castle closed for the season)

      What: Nynäs slott by Rundbosjön surrounded by the 3,830 ha Nynäs naturreservat — pastures, hollow oaks, forest and coastline; Sörmlandsleden stages 51–52 meet at the castle. STF's vandrarhem "Bränneriet" is on site.

      Drive: ~55–65 min, ~75–90 km via E4, Tystberga exit signed "Nynäs slott" (or coastal väg 219).

      November caveats (verified): the reserve, park grounds and trails are open year-round, but the castle, café, exhibitions and shop are closed until summer 2027 — bring food and water, no services on site; trails can be muddy; dark from ~15:45.

      Links: Google Maps · geo: — Nynäs slott and its signed parking

      Source: nynasslott.se — besöksinformation, sormlandsmuseum.se — Nynäs slott

      1.4 Bogesundslandet — peninsula hiking and a castle ruin

      What: 28.9 km² peninsula (half of Vaxholm kommun), a reserve since 2015, with marked walking/riding/cycling trails, bathing at Fridhemsbadet, and Bogesunds slott (1640s, Per Brahe d.y.) in the middle; STF Bogesund slottsvandrarhem is next door.

      Drive: ~45–55 min, ~40 km via väg 274 over the Vaxholm bridge, then Pålsundsvägen and Per Brahes väg to the castle parking.

      November caveats: the reserve is open year-round (verified), but there are no public castle tours in November — summer tours ran 6/6–13/9 2026 and the Christmas-market tours are 12–13 Dec 2026; the castle is a private residence, grounds open. Rocky shores icy — spikes; wind off Stora Värtan.

      Links: Google Maps · geo: — Bogesunds slott / Per Brahes väg parking

      Source: lansstyrelsen.se — Bogesundslandet, bogesundsslott.se

      2. Towns & castles

      2.1 Trosa — wooden town on the Trosaån, with an Advent Christmas market

      What: Picture-book small town at the mouth of Trosaån: Trosa Torg, Västra/Östra Långgatan, the old harbour and canal, medieval Trosa kyrka, and Garvaregården open-air museum next to the square.

      Drive: ~1 h 10 min, ~74 km via E4 south + väg 219.

      November caveats: the town is always accessible, but it is off-season — several summer restaurants close after September (e.g. Trosa Sjökrog) and others reduce to Fri–Sun (see §3.2 Kölsvinet). Verified event: Trosa julmarknad, Sunday 29 November 2026 (1st Advent), 10:00–15:00 at Trosa Torg/Garvaregården. Tourist office winter hours not verified.

      Links: Google Maps · geo: — Trosa Torg

      Source: trosa.com — Trosa julmarknad, trosa.com

      2.2 Nyköping — Nyköpingshus castle and Sörmlands museum

      What: Sörmland's county seat on the Nyköpingsån. Nyköpingshus (origins 1100s–1200s; the 1317 Nyköpings gästabud) with the white Kungstornet tower hosting three exhibitions; Sörmlands museum (Tolagsgatan 8, opposite the castle) has the main exhibitions, café and shop; stroll Åpromenaden and Norra Berget.

      Drive: ~1 h 10–20 min, ~90–102 km via E4 south.

      November caveats (verified): Sörmlands museum Tue–Sun 10:00–17:00, closed Mondays (Kulturkväll open to 21:00 on Wed 18 Nov 2026); Kungstornet Sundays 10:00–16:00 in the low season; julmarknad with FREE entry to both sites Sat 28 – Sun 29 Nov 2026. Ticket 80 kr (free ≤18) covers both sites one day. Castle ruins/tower are outdoors and unheated — dress warm. Regular guided-tour times in November not verified.

      Links: Google Maps · geo: — Nyköpingshus (museum parking on Tolagsgatan opposite)

      Source: sormlandsmuseum.se — Sörmlands museum, sormlandsmuseum.se — Nyköpingshus

      2.3 Söderköping — Göta kanal locks and a ghost of an ice-cream summer

      What: Medieval town where Göta kanal meets the Baltic: Storgatan's low wooden houses, the hand-operated canal locks, Rådhustorget and Ramunderberget with the view over town and archipelago.

      Drive: ~1 h 20–30 min, ~115–120 km via E4 south, Söderköping exit.

      November caveats: the town is open, but the famous Söderköpings Glass / Smultronstället is closed for the season (2026 season ended 30 Aug — verified) and the camping café closes 8 Nov 2026. Locks are not operating in winter. Year-round lunch options exist (Söderköpings Brunn, Österns Stjärna, Stockrosen Café) but their exact November hours were not verified. The adventsmarknad is the first December weekend — not in November.

      Links: Google Maps · geo: — Rådhustorget (canal locks: Maps · geo:)

      Source: smultronstallet.se — öppettider, soderkopingscamping.se, soderkoping.se

      2.4 Rosersbergs slott — Karl XIV Johan interiors (summer only) and a year-round park

      What: One of Sweden's best-preserved royal palaces (interiors nearly untouched from the Karl XIV Johan/Desideria era), with a baroque garden and English landscape park with views over Sigtunafjärden; the Ingegerdsleden trail starts here. Rosersbergs Slottshotell (hotel/restaurant/café) operates year-round.

      Drive: ~35 min, ~35 km via E4 north, exit 179 Rosersberg.

      November caveats (verified): the castle is closed for the 2026 season — guided tours run June–September only, and September–May the furnishings are winter-covered with no modern lighting, so no drop-in visits; groups can pre-book. The slottspark is open year-round — go midday, paths unlit and sunset ~16:00 in November. The Slottshotell's café/restaurant operates year-round (exact November hours not verified).

      Links: Google Maps · geo: — Rosersbergs slott, parking below the castle

      Source: kungligaslotten.se — Rosersbergs slott öppettider, rosersbergsslott.se

      2.5 Steninge slott & slottsby — manor park, shops and two Advent market weekends

      What: Late-17th-century manor (Nicodemus Tessin the Elder) by Lake Mälaren, ~1.5 km from Märsta. The castle is a private residence (interior closed year-round), but the slottspark is open to the public daily 12:00–16:30, and around it Steninge slottsby: Stenladan shops, Trälogen and Restaurang Steninge Bruk (see §3.4).

      Drive: ~40–45 min, ~45 km via E4 north, exit 180, väg 263 toward Märsta/Steninge.

      November caveats: "Jul i Stenladan" runs daily 8 Nov – 23 Dec 2026 (official window); julmarknad in Trälogen Sat 28 – Sun 29 Nov and 5–6 Dec 2026, 11:00–16:00 (arrive early for parking). Stenladan shops otherwise Mon–Fri 11:00–18:00, Sat–Sun 10:00–17:00. Park closes 16:30 — go midday.

      Links: Google Maps · geo: — Steninge slott, Steninge Slottsväg 141

      Source: steningeslottsby.se — Jul i Steninge slottsby, julmarknad.nu — Steninge slottsby, steningebruk.se

      3. Restaurants & cafés (food-first day trips)

      3.1 Taxinge-Näsby slott ("Kakslottet") — cake buffet in a castle, weekends only

      What: 17th–19th c. castle on a peninsula in Gripsholmsviken, run as a café with "the Nordic region's largest cake buffet" (~65 homemade cakes/breads, pay per item) plus smörgåsar and light dishes, served in the castle library or park. Slottslunch/menus are for pre-booked groups ≥15.

      Drive: ~50–60 min, ~58 km via E4/E20 south to the Nykvarn exit.

      November caveats (verified): the Slottscafé is open Fri–Sun 11:00–16:00 October–April — i.e. closed Mon–Thu in November — and closed Christmas/New Year. No November afternoon-tea dates were published (2026 season ended in October). Jul på Taxinge-Näsby Christmas market 13–15 and 19–22 Nov 2026 (entry fee those days, even for coffee only). Plan a Fri–Sun visit.

      Links: Google Maps · geo: — Taxinge-Näsby slott, Nykvarn

      Source: taxingeslott.se — öppettider, taxingeslott.se — slottscafé, taxingeslott.se — afternoon tea

      3.2 Kölsvinet, Trosa guest harbour — wood-fired pizza by the quay, Fri–Sun only

      What: Harbour-front restaurant/bar in Trosa gästhamn: wood-oven Neapolitan pizza, fish/seafood, terrace on the pontoons; lunch, dinner, events.

      Drive: ~55 min–1 h 10 min to Trosa (~65–74 km, E4 + väg 219).

      November caveats (verified): Fri–Sat 11:00–21:00, Sun 11:00–15:00, closed Mon–Thu — no seasonal note published, so these hours apply in November 2026; go Fri–Sun. Pair with a stroll in Trosa (§2.1; julmarknad 29 Nov). Note Trosa Sjökrog is closed for the season after September.

      Links: Google Maps · geo: — Kölsvinet, Uddbergagatan, Trosa gästhamn

      Source: kolsvinet.se, trosa.com — open all year, trosasjokrog.se — öppettider

      3.3 Kökeriet (Nynäs Rökeri) — smoked fish lunch in Nynäshamn harbour

      What: Harbour restaurant of the Nynäs Rökeri smokehouse, right on the fish quay in Nynäshamn: fish soup, baked haddock, hot-smoked salmon, räkmacka, weekday lunch; the fish shop next door sells smoked goods by weight.

      Drive: ~50 min, ~55–60 km via väg 73.

      November caveats (verified, November section of the official hours): Kökeriet closed Mondays; Tue–Fri 11:30–15:00, Sat 11:30–16:00, Sun 11:30–15:00 — lunch-only in November, so arrive early afternoon; Saturday is best. (Some Fridays are booked for private "räkfrossa".) The fish shop: Mon–Thu 10–18, Fri 9:30–18, Sat 10–16, Sun closed. Note Nynäs slott's own café is closed until summer 2027 — pair with a walk in the Nynäs reserve (§1.3) if you like.

      Links: Google Maps · geo: — Kökeriet, Fiskargränd 6, Nynäshamn

      Source: nynasrokeri.se — öppettider, nynasrokeri.se — Kökeriet

      3.4 Restaurang Steninge Bruk — lunch and weekend brunch in the old stone barn

      What: Restaurant in the stone barn at Steninge slottsby (Märsta): open-fire grill, lunch, weekend brunch, café.

      Drive: ~40–45 min, ~45 km via E4 north, exit 180 + väg 263.

      November caveats (standard hours published; no November deviations found — treat as verified pattern, not a November-specific guarantee): lunch Mon–Fri 11:00–14:30; brunch/helglunch Sat–Sun 10:30–16:00; café Mon–Fri 11:00–18:00 (Fri to 22:00), Sat–Sun 10:00–17:00. Combine with the slottspark (§2.5) — park closes 16:30 — and the julmarknad weekends 28–29 Nov.

      Links: Google Maps · geo: — Restaurang Steninge Bruk, Steninge Slottsväg 141, Märsta

      Source: steningebruk.se, steningeslottsby.se

      The standards you've already done (one-liners for November)

      • Mariefred (~1 h, E4): Gripsholms slott — check reduced winter tour hours on kungligaslotten.se before going; the town and Gripsholm's malmgård area are fine for a winter walk.
      • Sigtuna (~40 min, E4 norr): medieval town centre; most cafés/restaurants open year-round but with reduced winter hours — check individually.
      • Uppsala (~1 h, E4 norr): cathedral and university; museums keep normal winter hours (check each).
