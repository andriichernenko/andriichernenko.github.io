        1. Hikes & nature

        1.1 Järvafältet — the closest big landscape (open all winter)

        What: Large contiguous cultural-landscape reserve on Stockholm's northern edge: Iron-Age grave fields, open fields and forest; Östra Järvafältet's main entrance is at Bögs/Fäboda gård, Västra Järvafältet has its own entrances; Järvaleden runs through.

        Drive: ~25 min, ~18 km via E4 (Akalla/Hjulsta/Barkarby) or väg 267.

        November caveats: reserve open 24/7 year-round (verified). Exposed and windy in November; some paths muddy; no lighting after dark. Fire only at designated fireplaces.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Fäboda gård parking, main entrance

        Source: länsstyrelsen.se — Östra Järvafältet, länsstyrelsen.se — Västra Järvafältet

        1.2 Angarnssjöängen — the classic bird lake (open all winter)

        What: Premier birding lake (250+ species); the ~7 km Sjöängsslingan loops the lake on boardwalks, with fågeltorn (bird towers) and the Midsommarberget viewpoint. Main entrance/parking at Örsta (wheelchair platform, naturcentrum nearby); alternatives at Olhamra, Skesta hage and Kusta.

        Drive: ~35 min, ~30 km via E18 toward Vallentuna, then väg 273/255 to Örsta.

        November caveats: open 24/7 year-round (verified). Boardwalk sections can be wet/icy in November; no lighting after dark. Best birding is dawn–midday.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Entré Örsta parking

        Source: länsstyrelsen.se — Angarnssjöängen, naturkartan.se — parkering vid Örsta

        1.3 Paradiset — forest, lakes and a historic torp (open all winter)

        What: Paradiset-Hanveden, a large forest reserve in Huddinge's southern tip: old-growth patches, lakes Trekanten and Trehörningen with bathing rocks, marked trails (Sörmlandsleden passes through), six fireplaces and the old torp Paradiset with a raststuga.

        Drive: ~35 min, ~25–30 km via väg 259 (Haningeleden), exit toward "Ådran / Ekedal / Paradiset", then Ådravägen ~1.6 km to the main parking.

        November caveats: reserve open every day, all year (verified). The torp's raststuga is closed on Sundays in winter (reopens March) — bring your own fika and wood; fire only at the designated fireplaces. Headlamp recommended.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Entré Paradiset main parking

        Source: naturkartan.se — Entré Paradiset, länsstyrelsen.se — Paradiset, paradiset-hanveden.se

        1.4 Bogesundslandet — peninsula hiking and a castle (open all winter)

        What: 28.9 km² peninsula (half of Vaxholm kommun), a reserve since 2015, with marked walking/riding/cycling trails, bathing at Fridhemsbadet, and Bogesunds slott (1640s, Per Brahe d.y.) in the middle; STF Bogesund slottsvandrarhem is next door.

        Drive: ~50 min, ~40 km via väg 274 over the Vaxholm bridge, then Pålsundsvägen and Per Brahes väg to the castle parking.

        November caveats: reserve open year-round (verified). No public castle tours in November — summer tours ran 6/6–13/9 2026, Christmas-market tours 12–13 Dec 2026; the castle is a private residence, grounds open. Rocky shores icy — bring spikes; wind off Stora Värtan.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Bogesunds slott / Per Brahes väg parking

        Source: länsstyrelsen.se — Bogesundslandet, bogesundsslott.se

        1.5 Örbyhus slott park — castle by Vendelsjön (park open all winter)

        What: 15th-century castle on Vendelsjön surrounded by a lush park; the park and grounds are the November attraction — the castle interior is private/event-only.

        Drive: ~50 min, ~55 km via E4 north, then väg 600/76 toward Örbyhus.

        November caveats: park verified open year-round, free entrance; castle interior not open for drop-in (no November tours verified). Short daylight; unlit paths.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Örbyhus slott

        Source: orbyhusslott.se — parkerna, tierp.se

        1.6 Gimo bruk — ironworks village and mill pond (open all winter)

        What: Bruk village around Gimo herrgård and Gimo damm (bathing spot in summer, forest walks in winter); Upplandsleden stage 11 passes nearby. Parking at Gimo herrgård.

        Drive: ~55–60 min, ~80 km via väg 76 to Gimo (or E4 + väg 288).

        November caveats: outdoor walks always open (allemansrätten, verified via naturkartan); the herrgård interior/hotel restaurant is a separate stop (see §3.10). The damm is summer-only. Fire only at designated fireplaces.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Gimo herrgård parking

        Source: naturkartan.se — Parkering, Gimo herrgård, gimoherrgard.se

        1.7 Söderfors bruk & Engelska parken — riverside park with the Greek temple (open all winter)

        What: "The living bruk" by the Dalälven; the Engelska parken with the Greek temple (Grekiska templet) is a short signed round walk from the bruk; riverside strolls.

        Drive: ~60 min, ~75 km via väg 76 to Tierp, then Söderfors.

        November caveats: park/walk verified open year-round and free; the Erasteel works site is still operating — stay on marked paths. Cold by the Dalälven in November.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Söderfors herrgård / Engelska parken

        Source: länsstyrelsen.se — Engelska parken i Söderfors bruk, tierp.se — vandring, soderforsherrgard.se

        1.8 Torö & Ören — coastal island walks by road bridge (open all winter)

        What: Torö, an island reached by a fixed road bridge (open year-round), with the Ören nature reserve at its southern tip: Torö stenstrand, open-sea views toward Landsort, several signed free parking lots.

        Drive: ~60 min, ~58 km via väg 73 to Nynäshamn, over Älgviksbron and Herrhamra, then Torövägen.

        November caveats: reserve open 24/7 (verified); park only in designated lots; no open fires in Ören (allmänt eldningsförbud in the reserve per kommun); exposed and windy — spikes recommended.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Ören NR / Torö stenstrand, Torö's south tip

        Source: nynäshamn.se — Torö och Ören, naturkartan.se

        1.9 Nynäs — manor park and coastal trails (open all winter; castle closed)

        What: Nynäs slott by Rundbosjön surrounded by the 3,830 ha Nynäs naturreservat — pastures, hollow oaks, forest and coastline; Sörmlandsleden stages 51–52 meet at the castle; STF vandrarhem "Bränneriet" on site.

        Drive: ~60 min, ~75–90 km via E4, Tystberga exit signed "Nynäs slott" (or coastal väg 219).

        November caveats (verified): the reserve, park grounds and trails are open year-round, but the castle, café, exhibitions and shop are closed until summer 2027 — bring food and water; trails can be muddy; dark from ~15:00 in late November.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Nynäs slott and its signed parking

        Source: nynasslott.se — besöksinformation, sormlandsmuseum.se — Nynäs slott

        1.10 Grisslehamn — archipelago village at the end of Roslagsleden (open all winter)

        What: Small archipelago port at the end of väg 76/Roslagsleden: harbour, coastal path along the shore, motionsspår; Albert Engström's studio/gården (seasonal museum).

        Drive: ~65 min, ~75 km via väg 76.

        November caveats: coastal paths open year-round; museum, shops and cafés are seasonal — verify before relying on them. Exposed seafront: cold and windy in November.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Grisslehamn harbour / färjeläge

        Source: norrtalje.se, sv.wikipedia.org — Grisslehamn

        1.11 Femöre — coastal cliffs and a closed Cold War fort (open all winter; fort closed)

        What: 235 ha peninsula reserve south of Oxelösund: cliffs, sea-buckthorn, coastal pine forest; Sörmlandsleden's "Femöre runt" loops it. Under Femörehuvud sits Femörefortet, an intact 1966–2003 coastal artillery battery.

        Drive: ~85 min, ~120–125 km via E4 to Nyköping then väg 53 to Oxelösund, over Femörekanalen.

        November caveats: reserve open year-round (verified); the fort's last public 2026 tours ended 30 October — in November the fort interior is closed (group bookings only). Winter birding is good (sea eagles, eiders in the ice-free channel). Exposed cliffs: wind, spray ice, slippery rocks; never trust the ice in Femörekanalen. Fire only at designated grillplatser.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Femörefortets parkering (also fiskehamn parking: Maps · geo:)

        Source: länsstyrelsen.se — Femöre, femorefortet.se, visitoxelosund.se

        2. Towns & castles

        2.1 Rosersbergs slott — royal palace and park (park open all winter; interior closed)

        What: One of Sweden's best-preserved royal palaces (Karl XIV Johan/Desideria-era interiors), baroque garden and English landscape park with views over Sigtunafjärden; Ingegerdsleden starts here.

        Drive: ~35 min, ~35 km via E4 north, exit 179 Rosersberg.

        November caveats (verified): castle closed for the 2026 season — guided tours June–September only; furnishings winter-covered Sept–May, so no drop-in; groups can pre-book. Slottsparken open year-round — go midday, paths unlit. The Slottshotell restaurant/café is a separate food stop (§3.1).

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Rosersbergs slott, parking below the castle

        Source: kungligaslotten.se — Rosersbergs slott öppettider, rosersbergsslott.se

        2.2 Bålsta — Lions julmarknad on 2nd Advent (open all winter)

        What: Håbo kommun's centre by Lake Mälaren; a compact town centre with shops, cafés and restaurants around the centrum, ~15 min from Skoklosters slott.

        Drive: ~40 min, ~55 km via E18.

        November caveats: town and shops normal; Lions Julmarknad in Bålsta centrum Sun 6 Dec 2026, 10:00–15:00 (verified on balstacentrum.se — fish pond, lotteries, pony rides, local crafts); Luciatåg through the centre 13 Dec 13:00. Venue-level winter hours not verified.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Bålsta centrum

        Source: balstacentrum.se — Jul i Bålsta centrum

        2.3 Steninge slottsby — manor park, shops, Advent markets (open all winter)

        What: Late-17th-century manor (Tessin the Elder) by Lake Mälaren near Märsta. Castle interior is private year-round, but the slottspark is open daily 12:00–16:30; around it Steninge slottsby with Stenladan shops and Restaurang Steninge Bruk (§3.5).

        Drive: ~45 min, ~45 km via E4 north, exit 180, väg 263 toward Märsta/Steninge.

        November caveats: "Jul i Stenladan" daily 8 Nov – 23 Dec 2026 (official); julmarknad in Trälogen Sat 28 – Sun 29 Nov and 5–6 Dec 2026, 11:00–16:00 — arrive early for parking. Stenladan shops otherwise Mon–Fri 11:00–18:00, Sat–Sun 10:00–17:00. Park closes 16:30 — go midday.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Steninge slott, Steninge Slottsväg 141

        Source: steningeslottsby.se — Jul i Steninge slottsby, julmarknad.nu — Steninge slottsby, steningebruk.se

        2.4 Skoklosters slott — castle and Advent julmarknad (julmarknad 28–29/11 only)

        What: 17th-century castle on Lake Mälaren (one of the royal palaces); in November the castle interior is closed to drop-in (summer tours), but the julmarknad on 28–29 Nov 2026, 11:00–16:00 draws the season — market free, winter castle tour 95 kr.

        Drive: ~50 min, ~60 km via E18, then väg 255 toward Håbo/Skokloster.

        November caveats: outside the market weekend the castle is not open for tours (verified seasonal); the park/grounds remain walkable. Market weekends can be crowded — arrive before 11:00.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Skoklosters slott parking

        Source: skoklostersslott.se, bygdeband.se — julmarknader

        2.5 Enköping — "Pepparrotsstaden" and a traditional julmarknad (open all winter)

        What: Small city with Stora torget, the museum in a 1780s köpmansgård and old wooden quarters (Kaplanen, Rådhusgatan).

        Drive: ~57 min, ~80 km via E18.

        November caveats (verified): Enköpings museum Tue–Fri 10:00–16:00, Sat–Sun 11:00–15:00. Traditionell julmarknad on Stora torget/Kaplanen/museet, Sat 28 – Sun 29 Nov 2026 (Sat 10–16, Sun 11–16, free entry) — note one organiser page still showed stale 2025 dates; 28–29/11/2026 is correct for 2026.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Enköping Stora torget

        Source: enkoping.se — museum öppettider, lionsenkoping.se — julmarknad

        2.6 Strängnäs — cathedral city on Lake Mälaren (open all winter)

        What: Domkyrkan (one of Sweden's largest, richly decorated), cobbled stadskärna around Stortorget and Gripsholmsvägen, Mekanikus museum.

        Drive: ~60 min, ~95 km via E20 west.

        November caveats (verified): Strängnäs domkyrka open daily 10:00–16:00 (1 Sep–14 Jun). Julmarknad Sat 12 Dec 2026, 10:00–15:00 in the city centre, with sales in the cathedral 10:00–13:30. Dark early in November.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Strängnäs Stortorget/domkyrkan

        Source: svenskakyrkan.se — Strängnäs domkyrka, mittstrangnas.se — julmarknad

        2.7 Västerås + Anundshög — city winter and Sweden's largest burial mound (open all winter)

        What: Västerås: domkyrka, slott, Stora torget (winter ice rink when open). Anundshög ~10 min away: Sweden's largest tumulus plus Iron-Age grave field, free to explore; Café Anundshög open Tue–Sun 10:00–16:00 in the winter half-year.

        Drive: ~65 min to Västerås, ~105 km via E18; Anundshög +10 min.

        November caveats (verified): Anundshög grounds free year-round; café Tue–Sun 10–16 winter. Västerås City julmarknad Sun 22 Nov 2026, 11:00–16:00 on Stora/Bond-/Sigmatorget (official); Jul på Vallby friluftsmuseum 28–29 Nov, 11–16, free entry; Skultuna Messingsbruk julmarknad 20–22 Nov. Ice-rink opening date 2026 not verified.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Västerås Stora torget (Anundshög: Maps · geo:)

        Source: vasterascity.com — julmarknad 2026, anundshog.se, visitvasteras.se — julmarknader

        2.8 Trosa — wooden town with an Advent julmarknad (open all winter)

        What: Small town at the mouth of Trosaån: Trosa Torg, Västra/Östra Långgatan, the old harbour and canal, medieval Trosa kyrka, Garvaregården open-air museum by the square.

        Drive: ~70 min, ~74 km via E4 south + väg 219.

        November caveats: town always accessible, but off-season — several summer restaurants closed (e.g. Trosa Sjökrog) and others Fri–Sun only (see §3.9). Trosa julmarknad, Sun 29 Nov 2026 (1st Advent), 10:00–15:00, Trosa Torg/Garvaregården (verified). Tourist office winter hours not verified.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Trosa Torg

        Source: trosa.com — Trosa julmarknad, trosa.com

        2.9 Nyköping — Nyköpingshus castle and Sörmlands museum (open Tue–Sun)

        What: County seat on the Nyköpingsån. Nyköpingshus (1100s–1200s; the 1317 gästabud) with the white Kungstornet tower hosting three exhibitions; Sörmlands museum (Tolagsgatan 8, opposite the castle) has the main exhibitions, café and shop; stroll Åpromenaden and Norra Berget.

        Drive: ~75 min, ~90–102 km via E4 south.

        November caveats (verified): Sörmlands museum Tue–Sun 10:00–17:00, closed Mondays (Kulturkväll to 21:00 Wed 18 Nov 2026); Kungstornet Sundays 10:00–16:00 in the low season; julmarknad with FREE entry to both sites Sat 28 – Sun 29 Nov 2026. Ticket 80 kr (free ≤18), valid both sites one day. Castle ruins/tower outdoors and unheated — dress warm. Regular November tour times not verified.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Nyköpingshus (museum parking on Tolagsgatan opposite)

        Source: sormlandsmuseum.se — Sörmlands museum, sormlandsmuseum.se — Nyköpingshus

        2.10 Köping — market town on the E18 (open all winter)

        Drive: ~75 min, ~100 km via E18.

        November caveats: shops/restaurants keep normal hours (venue-level hours not verified). Julmarknad i Köping centrum Sat 28 Nov 2026, 10:00–15:00; julmarknad på Bürgers Växtgård (Barnhemsgatan 54) 21–22 Nov 2026, 10:00–16:00.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Köping Stora gatan

        Source: julmarknad.nu — Köping centrum, julmarknad.nu — Bürgers Växtgård

        2.11 Söderköping — Göta kanal locks (open all winter; glass scene closed)

        What: Medieval town where Göta kanal meets the Baltic: Storgatan's low wooden houses, the hand-operated canal locks, Rådhustorget and Ramunderberget with the view over town and archipelago.

        Drive: ~85 min, ~115–120 km via E4 south, Söderköping exit.

        November caveats: town open, but Söderköpings Glass / Smultronstället closed for the season (2026 season ended 30 Aug — verified) and the camping café closes 8 Nov 2026. Locks not operating in winter. Year-round lunch options exist (Söderköpings Brunn §3.12, Österns Stjärna, Stockrosen Café) but their exact November hours were not verified. Adventsmarknad is the first December weekend.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Rådhustorget (canal locks: Maps · geo:)

        Source: smultronstallet.se — öppettider, soderkopingscamping.se, soderkoping.se

        2.12 Sala silvergruva — 500-year silver mine (open daily, book ahead)

        What: Sala Silvergruva: guided tours 155 m down into the 500-year-old silver mine, museum, Konstmästargården; the town has its kyrka and Gruvfolkskör choir tradition.

        Drive: ~90 min, ~140–150 km via E18 → väg 70 (at the outer edge of the 1.5 h limit — leave early).

        November caveats (verified): mine tours every day (book via the calendar — popular slots sell out; age limit 5); Konstmästargården 10:00–16:00, museum 10:00–16:00. Lucia weekend 11–13 Dec 2026: julmarknad Sat 12 Dec + torch-lit mine descents (fackelvandringar), Lucia with Gruvfolkskör on 13 Dec. Outdoor area free but cold and dark early.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Sala silvergruva visitor parking

        Source: salasilvergruva.se — öppettider, salasilvergruva.se — jul/Lucia

        3. Restaurants, cafés, julbord

        3.1 Rosersbergs Slottshotell — slott's restaurant, julbord from 27/11 (partly closed: slottscafé closed Oct–Apr)

        What: Restaurant and hotel in the royal palace at Rosersberg: slottsfrukost, lunch, dinner; the slottscafé in the wing is closed October–April.

        Drive: ~35 min, ~25 km via E4 north, exit 176/179 Rosersberg.

        November caveats (verified): restaurant open — breakfast Tue–Fri 07:00–09:00, Sat–Sun 08:00–10:30; lunch Mon–Fri 11:30–13:00; dinner Mon–Sat 17:30–20:00 — but in autumn/winter it mainly runs on weekends, meetings, events and julbord, so always book a table. Julbord 27 Nov – 19 Dec 2026: evening sittings Wed–Sat 17:30 & 19:30 (795 kr Wed–Thu, 895 kr Fri, 995 kr Sat), lunch sittings Sat–Sun 13:00 (895 kr); vegan/vegetarian 50%. Slottscafé closed October–April.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Rosersbergs slott / Slottshotell, Slottsvägen 203

        Source: rosersbergsslott.se — julbord, rosersbergsslott.se

        3.2 Bommersvik — castle julbord by lake Yngern (julbord-only stop; weekends OK)

        What: Conference castle/hotel in Nykvarn kommun by Lake Yngern, ~40 min south. In November–December the regular restaurant is closed and the venue runs its julbord — buffet with 40 hot/cold dishes, glöggmingel, cheese and dessert buffet; Sunday is a family-lunch julbord with julpyssel for children.

        Drive: ~40 min, ~40 km via E4 south, Nykvarn/Järna area.

        November caveats (verified): julbord Thu–Sun 26 Nov – 19 Dec 2026 (weeks 47–50; week 51 Mon–Wed); 795 kr Thu–Sat, Sunday family lunch; book ahead. Outside julbord sittings the restaurant is closed or on request only — this is a julbord day trip, not a general lunch stop.

        Links: Google Maps · geo: — Bommersvik

        Suggested 1-day itineraries (weekends)

        One main destination per day, plus at most one meal stop. Leave Stockholm by ~09:00–09:30 and plan to be back before sunset (see the daylight table: 15:38 on 8 Nov, 14:47 from 13 Dec). All entries referenced by their section numbers above.

        I1. Sun 22 Nov — Västerås winter day: City julmarknad + Anundshög (§2.7)

        Drive ~65 min. Stora torget's City julmarknad 11:00–16:00 (free), then Anundshög (~10 min): Sweden's largest burial mound and grave field, free to explore; Café Anundshög open Tue–Sun 10:00–16:00. Back before 15:09 sunset.

        I2. Sat 28 – Sun 29 Nov — Nyköping: castle, museum and free-entry julmarknad (§2.9)

        Drive ~75 min. Sörmlands museum + Nyköpingshus/Kungstornet have FREE entry and a julmarknad on 28–29 Nov; museum Tue–Sun 10:00–17:00, Kungstornet Sun 10:00–16:00. Lunch at the museum café. Ticket otherwise 80 kr covers both sites.

        I3. Sat 28 – Sun 29 Nov — Enköping: traditional julmarknad + museum + lunch (§2.5, §3.8)

        Drive ~57 min. Julmarknad on Stora torget/Kaplanen Sat 10–16, Sun 11–16 (free); Enköpings museum Sat–Sun 11:00–15:00; lunch at Mel's Garage (open daily; Sat 09:00–16:00).

        I4. Sun 29 Nov — Trosa Advent market + harbour lunch (§2.8, §3.9)

        Drive ~70 min. Trosa julmarknad 10:00–15:00 at Trosa Torg/Garvaregården, town stroll, then lunch at Kölsvinet in the guest harbour (open Fri–Sun, 11:00–21:00).

        I5. Sat 28 – Sun 29 Nov — Skokloster Advent market + Wira bruk (§2.4, §3.4)

        Drive ~50 min to Skoklosters slott (julmarknad 28–29 Nov, 11:00–16:00, market free; winter castle tour 95 kr), then ~20 min to Wira bruk: its Sagolik Julmarknad runs 27–29 Nov and Wira Restaurang is open Sat–Sun 11:00–16:00.

        I6. Fri–Sun in November — Taxinge-Näsby: cake buffet, julmarknad or julbord (§3.7)

        Drive ~55 min. Slottscafé open Fri–Sun 11:00–16:00; Jul på Taxinge-Näsby 13–15 and 19–22 Nov (entry fee); julbord 27 Nov – 20 Dec — book a Sat/Sun sitting.

        I7. Weekend morning hike + smokehouse lunch — Torö/Ören + Nynäshamn (§1.8, §3.6)

        Drive ~60 min to Torö (Ören NR, south tip — coastal walk), then down to Nynäshamn for lunch at Kökeriet (Sat 11:30–16:00, Sun 11:30–15:00). On 5–6 Dec add Nynäshamn's julmarknad in the centre.

        I8. Weekend julbord evenings (book ahead) (§3.1, §3.2, §3.7, §3.10, §3.11, §3.12)

        Weekend sittings within 1.5 h: Rosersbergs Slottshotell Sat–Sun lunch 13:00 (895 kr) or Sat evening 17:30/19:30, 27 Nov–19 Dec · Bommersvik Sat/Sun, Thu–Sun 26 Nov–19 Dec, 795 kr · Taxinge-Näsby Sat/Sun sittings, 27 Nov–20 Dec · Gimo herrgård Sat–Sun dinner, 22 Nov–22 Dec · Söderfors herrgård Sat/Sun, 27 Nov–20 Dec · Söderköpings Brunn Sunday julbord only, 22 Nov–20 Dec.

        I9. Sat 12 Dec — Sala: mine tour + Lucia julmarknad (§2.12)

        Drive ~90 min (leave early). Book a Sala silvergruva tour (daily; 155 m down), then the Lucia weekend julmarknad Sat 12 Dec with torch-lit mine descents (fackelvandringar). Sunset 14:47 — plan the mine tour for the morning.

        I10. Sun 6 Dec — Bålsta Lions julmarknad + short nearby walk (§2.2, §1.5)

        Drive ~40 min. Lions Julmarknad in Bålsta centrum 10:00–15:00 (fish pond, pony rides, local crafts); afterwards a short park walk at Örbyhus slott (~15 min away, park open all winter). Luciatåg through Bålsta on 13 Dec at 13:00 if you stay.
