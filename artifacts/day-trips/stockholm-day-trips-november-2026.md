# Day trips by car from Stockholm — November 2026

Lesser-known destinations within ~1.5 hours' drive of T-Centralen, for a month with short days,
off-season opening hours and the first snow/ice. One main destination per day — no multi-stop loops.
Daylight in November 2026: 1 Nov sunrise ≈ 07:00 / sunset ≈ 16:35, by 30 Nov ≈ 08:16 / 15:25 — plan
outdoor time roughly 09:00–15:00 and bring a headlamp. All opening hours below were checked on
official sites on **2026-11-07**; anything that could not be verified is listed at the end. Every
entry has a Google Maps link and a `geo:` link with GPS coordinates taken from the Google Maps pin of
the entrance / parking lot / POI itself (all within ~200 m of the actual spot).

## 1. Hikes & nature

### 1.1 Paradiset nature reserve — forest, lakes and a historic torp
**What:** Paradiset-Hanveden, a large forest reserve in Huddinge's southern tip: old-growth patches,
the lakes Trekanten and Trehörningen with bathing rocks, several marked trails (Sörmlandsleden passes
through), six fireplaces and the old torp Paradiset with a raststuga.

**Drive:** ~30–40 min, ~25–30 km via väg 259 (Haningeleden), exit toward "Ådran / Ekedal / Paradiset",
then Ådravägen ~1.6 km to the main parking.

**November caveats:** the reserve is open every day, all year (verified). The torp's raststuga is
**closed on Sundays in winter** (reopens in March), so bring your own fika; fire only at the
designated fireplaces (bring your own wood). Short daylight — headlamp recommended.

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=59.159899,18.046202) · [geo:](geo:59.159899,18.046202) — Entré Paradiset main parking

**Source:** [naturkartan.se — Entré Paradiset](https://www.naturkartan.se/sv/stockholms-lan/entre-paradiset-paradisets-naturreservat), [lansstyrelsen.se — Paradiset](https://www.lansstyrelsen.se/stockholm/besoksmal/naturreservat/paradiset.html), [paradiset-hanveden.se](https://paradiset-hanveden.se/)

### 1.2 Femöre peninsula — coastal cliffs, and a closed Cold War fort
**What:** 235 ha reserve on the peninsula south of Oxelösund: cliffs, sea-buckthorn, coastal pine
forest; the Sörmlandsleden "Femöre runt" loop goes around it. Under Femörehuvud sits **Femörefortet**,
an intact 1966–2003 coastal artillery battery blasted into the rock.

**Drive:** ~1 h 20–30 min, ~120–125 km via E4 to Nyköping then väg 53 to Oxelösund, over Femörekanalen.

**November caveats:** the reserve is **open year-round** (verified), but the fort's last public 2026
tours ended 30 October — **in November the fort interior is closed** (group bookings only). Winter
birding is good (sea eagles, eiders in the ice-free channel). Exposed cliffs: wind, spray ice,
slippery rocks; never trust the ice in Femörekanalen. Fire only at designated grillplatser.

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=58.653173,17.107627) · [geo:](geo:58.653173,17.107627) — Femörefortets parkering (stora parkeringen; also fiskehamn parking: [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=58.655962,17.099209) · [geo:](geo:58.655962,17.099209))

**Source:** [lansstyrelsen.se — Femöre](https://www.lansstyrelsen.se/sodermanland/besoksmal/naturreservat/femore.html), [femorefortet.se](https://www.femorefortet.se/), [visitoxelosund.se](https://visitoxelosund.se/)

### 1.3 Nynäs — manor park and coastal trails (castle closed for the season)
**What:** Nynäs slott by Rundbosjön surrounded by the 3,830 ha Nynäs naturreservat — pastures, hollow
oaks, forest and coastline; Sörmlandsleden stages 51–52 meet at the castle. STF's vandrarhem
"Bränneriet" is on site.

**Drive:** ~55–65 min, ~75–90 km via E4, Tystberga exit signed "Nynäs slott" (or coastal väg 219).

**November caveats (verified):** the **reserve, park grounds and trails are open year-round**, but the
**castle, café, exhibitions and shop are closed until summer 2027** — bring food and water, no
services on site; trails can be muddy; dark from ~15:45.

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=58.807945,17.366226) · [geo:](geo:58.807945,17.366226) — Nynäs slott and its signed parking

**Source:** [nynasslott.se — besöksinformation](https://nynasslott.se/besoksinformation/), [sormlandsmuseum.se — Nynäs slott](https://sormlandsmuseum.se/besoksinfo/nynas-slott/)

### 1.4 Bogesundslandet — peninsula hiking and a castle ruin
**What:** 28.9 km² peninsula (half of Vaxholm kommun), a reserve since 2015, with marked
walking/riding/cycling trails, bathing at Fridhemsbadet, and **Bogesunds slott** (1640s, Per Brahe d.y.)
in the middle; STF Bogesund slottsvandrarhem is next door.

**Drive:** ~45–55 min, ~40 km via väg 274 over the Vaxholm bridge, then Pålsundsvägen and Per Brahes
väg to the castle parking.

**November caveats:** the reserve is **open year-round** (verified), but there are **no public castle
tours in November** — summer tours ran 6/6–13/9 2026 and the Christmas-market tours are 12–13 Dec 2026;
the castle is a private residence, grounds open. Rocky shores icy — spikes; wind off Stora Värtan.

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=59.394016,18.286209) · [geo:](geo:59.394016,18.286209) — Bogesunds slott / Per Brahes väg parking

**Source:** [lansstyrelsen.se — Bogesundslandet](https://www.lansstyrelsen.se/stockholm/besoksmal/naturreservat/bogesundslandet.html), [bogesundsslott.se](https://bogesundsslott.se/slottet-idag)

## 2. Towns & castles

### 2.1 Trosa — wooden town on the Trosaån, with an Advent Christmas market
**What:** Picture-book small town at the mouth of Trosaån: Trosa Torg, Västra/Östra Långgatan, the old
harbour and canal, medieval Trosa kyrka, and Garvaregården open-air museum next to the square.

**Drive:** ~1 h 10 min, ~74 km via E4 south + väg 219.

**November caveats:** the town is always accessible, but it is off-season — several summer
restaurants close after September (e.g. Trosa Sjökrog) and others reduce to Fri–Sun (see §3.2
Kölsvinet). Verified event: **Trosa julmarknad, Sunday 29 November 2026 (1st Advent), 10:00–15:00**
at Trosa Torg/Garvaregården. Tourist office winter hours not verified.

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=58.895934,17.547115) · [geo:](geo:58.895934,17.547115) — Trosa Torg

**Source:** [trosa.com — Trosa julmarknad](https://trosa.com/event/trosa-julmarknad/), [trosa.com](https://trosa.com/)

### 2.2 Nyköping — Nyköpingshus castle and Sörmlands museum
**What:** Sörmland's county seat on the Nyköpingsån. **Nyköpingshus** (origins 1100s–1200s; the 1317
Nyköpings gästabud) with the white Kungstornet tower hosting three exhibitions; **Sörmlands museum**
(Tolagsgatan 8, opposite the castle) has the main exhibitions, café and shop; stroll Åpromenaden and
Norra Berget.

**Drive:** ~1 h 10–20 min, ~90–102 km via E4 south.

**November caveats (verified):** Sörmlands museum **Tue–Sun 10:00–17:00, closed Mondays**
(Kulturkväll open to 21:00 on Wed 18 Nov 2026); **Kungstornet Sundays 10:00–16:00** in the low season;
**julmarknad with FREE entry to both sites Sat 28 – Sun 29 Nov 2026**. Ticket 80 kr (free ≤18) covers
both sites one day. Castle ruins/tower are outdoors and unheated — dress warm. Regular guided-tour
times in November not verified.

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=58.748889,17.011111) · [geo:](geo:58.748889,17.011111) — Nyköpingshus (museum parking on Tolagsgatan opposite)

**Source:** [sormlandsmuseum.se — Sörmlands museum](https://sormlandsmuseum.se/besoksinfo/sormlands-museum/), [sormlandsmuseum.se — Nyköpingshus](https://sormlandsmuseum.se/besoksinfo/nykopingshus/)

### 2.3 Söderköping — Göta kanal locks and a ghost of an ice-cream summer
**What:** Medieval town where Göta kanal meets the Baltic: Storgatan's low wooden houses, the
hand-operated canal locks, Rådhustorget and Ramunderberget with the view over town and archipelago.

**Drive:** ~1 h 20–30 min, ~115–120 km via E4 south, Söderköping exit.

**November caveats:** the town is open, but the famous **Söderköpings Glass / Smultronstället is
closed for the season (2026 season ended 30 Aug — verified)** and the camping café closes 8 Nov 2026.
Locks are not operating in winter. Year-round lunch options exist (Söderköpings Brunn, Österns Stjärna,
Stockrosen Café) but their exact November hours were **not verified**. The adventsmarknad is the first
December weekend — not in November.

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=58.481977,16.320255) · [geo:](geo:58.481977,16.320255) — Rådhustorget (canal locks: [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=58.488693,16.295662) · [geo:](geo:58.488693,16.295662))

**Source:** [smultronstallet.se — öppettider](https://www.smultronstallet.se/oppettider/), [soderkopingscamping.se](https://www.soderkopingscamping.se/), [soderkoping.se](https://www.soderkoping.se/)

### 2.4 Rosersbergs slott — Karl XIV Johan interiors (summer only) and a year-round park
**What:** One of Sweden's best-preserved royal palaces (interiors nearly untouched from the
Karl XIV Johan/Desideria era), with a baroque garden and English landscape park with views over
Sigtunafjärden; the Ingegerdsleden trail starts here. Rosersbergs Slottshotell (hotel/restaurant/café)
operates year-round.

**Drive:** ~35 min, ~35 km via E4 north, exit 179 Rosersberg.

**November caveats (verified):** the castle is **closed for the 2026 season — guided tours run
June–September only**, and September–May the furnishings are winter-covered with no modern lighting,
so no drop-in visits; groups can pre-book. The **slottspark is open year-round** — go midday, paths
unlit and sunset ~16:00 in November. The Slottshotell's café/restaurant operates year-round (exact
November hours not verified).

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=59.574951,17.843381) · [geo:](geo:59.574951,17.843381) — Rosersbergs slott, parking below the castle

**Source:** [kungligaslotten.se — Rosersbergs slott öppettider](https://www.kungligaslotten.se/vara-besoksmal/rosersbergs-slott/oppettider.html), [rosersbergsslott.se](https://www.rosersbergsslott.se/)

### 2.5 Steninge slott & slottsby — manor park, shops and two Advent market weekends
**What:** Late-17th-century manor (Nicodemus Tessin the Elder) by Lake Mälaren, ~1.5 km from Märsta.
The castle is a private residence (interior closed year-round), but the **slottspark is open to the
public daily 12:00–16:30**, and around it Steninge slottsby: Stenladan shops, Trälogen and
**Restaurang Steninge Bruk** (see §3.4).

**Drive:** ~40–45 min, ~45 km via E4 north, exit 180, väg 263 toward Märsta/Steninge.

**November caveats:** **"Jul i Stenladan" runs daily 8 Nov – 23 Dec 2026** (official window);
**julmarknad in Trälogen Sat 28 – Sun 29 Nov and 5–6 Dec 2026, 11:00–16:00** (arrive early for
parking). Stenladan shops otherwise Mon–Fri 11:00–18:00, Sat–Sun 10:00–17:00. Park closes 16:30 — go
midday.

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=59.603754,17.802121) · [geo:](geo:59.603754,17.802121) — Steninge slott, Steninge Slottsväg 141

**Source:** [steningeslottsby.se — Jul i Steninge slottsby](https://steningeslottsby.se/fira-jul-i-steninge-slottsby/), [julmarknad.nu — Steninge slottsby](https://www.julmarknad.nu/event/julmarknad-steninge-slottsby/), [steningebruk.se](https://www.steningebruk.se/)

## 3. Restaurants & cafés (food-first day trips)

### 3.1 Taxinge-Näsby slott ("Kakslottet") — cake buffet in a castle, weekends only
**What:** 17th–19th c. castle on a peninsula in Gripsholmsviken, run as a café with "the Nordic region's
largest cake buffet" (~65 homemade cakes/breads, pay per item) plus smörgåsar and light dishes, served
in the castle library or park. Slottslunch/menus are for pre-booked groups ≥15.

**Drive:** ~50–60 min, ~58 km via E4/E20 south to the Nykvarn exit.

**November caveats (verified):** the Slottscafé is open Fri–Sun 11:00–16:00 October–April — i.e.
**closed Mon–Thu in November** — and closed Christmas/New Year. No November afternoon-tea dates were
published (2026 season ended in October). Jul på Taxinge-Näsby Christmas market 13–15 and 19–22 Nov
2026 (entry fee those days, even for coffee only). Plan a Fri–Sun visit.

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=59.246944,17.309167) · [geo:](geo:59.246944,17.309167) — Taxinge-Näsby slott, Nykvarn

**Source:** [taxingeslott.se — öppettider](https://www.taxingeslott.se/oppettider/), [taxingeslott.se — slottscafé](https://www.taxingeslott.se/slottscafe/), [taxingeslott.se — afternoon tea](https://www.taxingeslott.se/afternoon-tea/)

### 3.2 Kölsvinet, Trosa guest harbour — wood-fired pizza by the quay, Fri–Sun only
**What:** Harbour-front restaurant/bar in Trosa gästhamn: wood-oven Neapolitan pizza, fish/seafood,
terrace on the pontoons; lunch, dinner, events.

**Drive:** ~55 min–1 h 10 min to Trosa (~65–74 km, E4 + väg 219).

**November caveats (verified):** **Fri–Sat 11:00–21:00, Sun 11:00–15:00, closed Mon–Thu** — no seasonal
note published, so these hours apply in November 2026; go Fri–Sun. Pair with a stroll in Trosa (§2.1;
julmarknad 29 Nov). Note Trosa Sjökrog is **closed for the season** after September.

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=58.890812,17.552446) · [geo:](geo:58.890812,17.552446) — Kölsvinet, Uddbergagatan, Trosa gästhamn

**Source:** [kolsvinet.se](https://www.kolsvinet.se/), [trosa.com — open all year](https://trosa.com/en/eat-drink/open-all-year/), [trosasjokrog.se — öppettider](https://www.trosasjokrog.se/oppettider/)

### 3.3 Kökeriet (Nynäs Rökeri) — smoked fish lunch in Nynäshamn harbour
**What:** Harbour restaurant of the Nynäs Rökeri smokehouse, right on the fish quay in Nynäshamn:
fish soup, baked haddock, hot-smoked salmon, räkmacka, weekday lunch; the fish shop next door sells
smoked goods by weight.

**Drive:** ~50 min, ~55–60 km via väg 73.

**November caveats (verified, November section of the official hours):** Kökeriet **closed Mondays;
Tue–Fri 11:30–15:00, Sat 11:30–16:00, Sun 11:30–15:00** — lunch-only in November, so arrive early
afternoon; Saturday is best. (Some Fridays are booked for private "räkfrossa".) The fish shop:
Mon–Thu 10–18, Fri 9:30–18, Sat 10–16, Sun closed. Note **Nynäs slott's own café is closed until summer
2027** — pair with a walk in the Nynäs reserve (§1.3) if you like.

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=58.900869,17.952397) · [geo:](geo:58.900869,17.952397) — Kökeriet, Fiskargränd 6, Nynäshamn

**Source:** [nynasrokeri.se — öppettider](https://www.nynasrokeri.se/oppetider), [nynasrokeri.se — Kökeriet](https://www.nynasrokeri.se/kokeriet/)

### 3.4 Restaurang Steninge Bruk — lunch and weekend brunch in the old stone barn
**What:** Restaurant in the stone barn at Steninge slottsby (Märsta): open-fire grill, lunch,
weekend brunch, café.

**Drive:** ~40–45 min, ~45 km via E4 north, exit 180 + väg 263.

**November caveats (standard hours published; no November deviations found — treat as verified
pattern, not a November-specific guarantee):** lunch Mon–Fri 11:00–14:30; **brunch/helglunch Sat–Sun
10:30–16:00**; café Mon–Fri 11:00–18:00 (Fri to 22:00), Sat–Sun 10:00–17:00. Combine with the slottspark
(§2.5) — park closes 16:30 — and the julmarknad weekends 28–29 Nov.

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=59.605194,17.800427) · [geo:](geo:59.605194,17.800427) — Restaurang Steninge Bruk, Steninge Slottsväg 141, Märsta

**Source:** [steningebruk.se](https://www.steningebruk.se/), [steningeslottsby.se](https://www.steningeslottsby.se/)

## The standards you've already done (one-liners for November)

- **Mariefred** (~1 h, E4): Gripsholms slott — check reduced winter tour hours on kungligaslotten.se
  before going; the town and Gripsholm's malmgård area are fine for a winter walk.
- **Sigtuna** (~40 min, E4 norr): medieval town centre; most cafés/restaurants open year-round but with
  reduced winter hours — check individually.
- **Uppsala** (~1 h, E4 norr): cathedral and university; museums keep normal winter hours (check each).
- **Drottningholm** (~30 min): palace park open year-round; the palace and slottsteater have off-season
  hours — closed or guided-only in November (kungligaslotten.se).
- **Waxholm** (ferry from Strömkajen or car via väg 274): Vaxholms fästning museum has reduced winter
  hours; Waxholmsbolaget switches to the winter timetable on 10 Dec 2026 and already runs reduced
  service in November — check departures at waxholmsbolaget.se before you go.
