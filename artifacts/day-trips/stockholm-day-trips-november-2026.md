# Day trips by car from Stockholm — November–December 2026

Lesser-known destinations within ~1.5 hours' drive of T-Centralen, for the short days and off-season
of late autumn. One main destination per day — no multi-stop loops. The guide is **weekend-focused**:
what matters is whether a place is open on **Sat–Sun**; weekday-only service is flagged. Entries in each
section are sorted by driving time, shortest first; headings note whether the destination is **open all
winter** or **partly closed** in November–December. Opening hours were checked on official sites on
**2026-11-07**; anything that could not be verified is listed at the end.

## Daylight in November–December 2026

Plan outdoor time roughly 09:00–15:00 in November and bring a headlamp — by mid-December the sun sets
before 15:00.

## 1. Hikes & nature

### 1.1 Järvafältet — the closest big landscape (**open all winter**)
**What:** Large contiguous cultural-landscape reserve on Stockholm's northern edge: Iron-Age grave
fields, open fields and forest; Östra Järvafältet's main entrance is at Bögs/Fäboda gård, Västra
Järvafältet has its own entrances; Järvaleden runs through.

**Drive:** ~25 min, ~18 km via E4 (Akalla/Hjulsta/Barkarby) or väg 267.

**November caveats:** reserve open 24/7 year-round (verified). Exposed and windy in November; some
paths muddy; no lighting after dark. Fire only at designated fireplaces.

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=59.463087,17.870929) · [geo:](geo:59.463087,17.870929) — Fäboda gård parking, main entrance

**Source:** [länsstyrelsen.se — Östra Järvafältet](https://www.lansstyrelsen.se/stockholm/besoksmal/naturreservat/ostra-jarvafaltet.html), [länsstyrelsen.se — Västra Järvafältet](https://www.lansstyrelsen.se/stockholm/besoksmal/naturreservat/vastra-jarvafaltet.html)

### 1.2 Angarnssjöängen — the classic bird lake (**open all winter**)
**What:** Premier birding lake (250+ species); the ~7 km Sjöängsslingan loops the lake on boardwalks,
with fågeltorn (bird towers) and the Midsommarberget viewpoint. Main entrance/parking at Örsta
(wheelchair platform, naturcentrum nearby); alternatives at Olhamra, Skesta hage and Kusta.

**Drive:** ~35 min, ~30 km via E18 toward Vallentuna, then väg 273/255 to Örsta.

**November caveats:** open 24/7 year-round (verified). Boardwalk sections can be wet/icy in November;
no lighting after dark. Best birding is dawn–midday.

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=59.538917,18.150240) · [geo:](geo:59.538917,18.150240) — Entré Örsta parking

**Source:** [länsstyrelsen.se — Angarnssjöängen](https://www.lansstyrelsen.se/stockholm/besoksmal/naturreservat/angarnssjoangen.html), [naturkartan.se — parkering vid Örsta](https://www.naturkartan.se/en/stockholms-lan/parkering-vid-orsta)

### 1.3 Paradiset — forest, lakes and a historic torp (**open all winter**)
**What:** Paradiset-Hanveden, a large forest reserve in Huddinge's southern tip: old-growth patches,
lakes Trekanten and Trehörningen with bathing rocks, marked trails (Sörmlandsleden passes through),
six fireplaces and the old torp Paradiset with a raststuga.

**Drive:** ~35 min, ~25–30 km via väg 259 (Haningeleden), exit toward "Ådran / Ekedal / Paradiset",
then Ådravägen ~1.6 km to the main parking.

**November caveats:** reserve open every day, all year (verified). The torp's raststuga is **closed on
Sundays in winter** (reopens March) — bring your own fika and wood; fire only at the designated
fireplaces. Headlamp recommended.

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=59.159899,18.046202) · [geo:](geo:59.159899,18.046202) — Entré Paradiset main parking

**Source:** [naturkartan.se — Entré Paradiset](https://www.naturkartan.se/sv/stockholms-lan/entre-paradiset-paradisets-naturreservat), [länsstyrelsen.se — Paradiset](https://www.lansstyrelsen.se/stockholm/besoksmal/naturreservat/paradiset.html), [paradiset-hanveden.se](https://paradiset-hanveden.se/)

### 1.4 Bogesundslandet — peninsula hiking and a castle (**open all winter**)
**What:** 28.9 km² peninsula (half of Vaxholm kommun), a reserve since 2015, with marked
walking/riding/cycling trails, bathing at Fridhemsbadet, and **Bogesunds slott** (1640s, Per Brahe d.y.)
in the middle; STF Bogesund slottsvandrarhem is next door.

**Drive:** ~50 min, ~40 km via väg 274 over the Vaxholm bridge, then Pålsundsvägen and Per Brahes
väg to the castle parking.

**November caveats:** reserve open year-round (verified). **No public castle tours in November** —
summer tours ran 6/6–13/9 2026, Christmas-market tours 12–13 Dec 2026; the castle is a private
residence, grounds open. Rocky shores icy — bring spikes; wind off Stora Värtan.

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=59.394016,18.286209) · [geo:](geo:59.394016,18.286209) — Bogesunds slott / Per Brahes väg parking

**Source:** [länsstyrelsen.se — Bogesundslandet](https://www.lansstyrelsen.se/stockholm/besoksmal/naturreservat/bogesundslandet.html), [bogesundsslott.se](https://bogesundsslott.se/slottet-idag)

### 1.5 Örbyhus slott park — castle by Vendelsjön (**park open all winter**)
**What:** 15th-century castle on Vendelsjön surrounded by a lush park; the park and grounds are the
November attraction — the castle interior is private/event-only.

**Drive:** ~50 min, ~55 km via E4 north, then väg 600/76 toward Örbyhus.

**November caveats:** park verified open year-round, free entrance; castle interior not open for
drop-in (no November tours verified). Short daylight; unlit paths.

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=60.200007,17.709127) · [geo:](geo:60.200007,17.709127) — Örbyhus slott

**Source:** [orbyhusslott.se — parkerna](https://www.orbyhusslott.se/parkerna), [tierp.se](https://www.tierp.se/)

### 1.6 Gimo bruk — ironworks village and mill pond (**open all winter**)
**What:** Bruk village around Gimo herrgård and Gimo damm (bathing spot in summer, forest walks in
winter); Upplandsleden stage 11 passes nearby. Parking at Gimo herrgård.

**Drive:** ~55–60 min, ~80 km via väg 76 to Gimo (or E4 + väg 288).

**November caveats:** outdoor walks always open (allemansrätten, verified via naturkartan); the herrgård
interior/hotel restaurant is a separate stop (see §3.10). The damm is summer-only. Fire only at
designated fireplaces.

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=60.178311,18.173202) · [geo:](geo:60.178311,18.173202) — Gimo herrgård parking

**Source:** [naturkartan.se — Parkering, Gimo herrgård](https://www.naturkartan.se/en/uppsala-lan/parkering-gimo-herrgard), [gimoherrgard.se](https://www.gimoherrgard.se/)

### 1.7 Söderfors bruk & Engelska parken — riverside park with the Greek temple (**open all winter**)
**What:** "The living bruk" by the Dalälven; the **Engelska parken** with the Greek temple (Grekiska
templet) is a short signed round walk from the bruk; riverside strolls.

**Drive:** ~60 min, ~75 km via väg 76 to Tierp, then Söderfors.

**November caveats:** park/walk verified open year-round and free; the Erasteel works site is still
operating — stay on marked paths. Cold by the Dalälven in November.

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=60.383427,17.238822) · [geo:](geo:60.383427,17.238822) — Söderfors herrgård / Engelska parken

### 1.8 Torö & Ören — coastal island walks by road bridge (**open all winter**)
**What:** Torö, an island reached by a fixed road bridge (open year-round), with the Ören nature
reserve at its southern tip: Torö stenstrand, open-sea views toward Landsort, several signed free
parking lots.

**Drive:** ~60 min, ~58 km via väg 73 to Nynäshamn, over Älgviksbron and Herrhamra, then Torövägen.

**November caveats:** reserve open 24/7 (verified); park only in designated lots; **no open fires** in
Ören (allmänt eldningsförbud in the reserve per kommun); exposed and windy — spikes recommended.

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=58.801900,17.688700) · [geo:](geo:58.801900,17.688700) — Ören NR, Torö's south tip

**Source:** [nynäshamn.se — Torö och Ören](https://www.nynas.se/uppleva/skargard--batliv/toro-och-oren), [naturkartan.se](https://www.naturkartan.se/)

### 1.9 Nynäs — manor park and coastal trails (**open all winter**; castle closed)
**What:** Nynäs slott by Rundbosjön surrounded by the 3,830 ha Nynäs naturreservat — pastures, hollow
oaks, forest and coastline; Sörmlandsleden stages 51–52 meet at the castle; STF vandrarhem
"Bränneriet" on site.

**Drive:** ~60 min, ~75–90 km via E4, Tystberga exit signed "Nynäs slott" (or coastal väg 219).

**November caveats (verified):** the **reserve, park grounds and trails are open year-round**, but the
**castle, café, exhibitions and shop are closed until summer 2027** — bring food and water; trails can
be muddy; dark from ~15:00 in late November.

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=58.807945,17.366226) · [geo:](geo:58.807945,17.366226) — Nynäs slott and its signed parking

**Source:** [nynasslott.se — besöksinformation](https://nynasslott.se/besoksinformation/), [sormlandsmuseum.se — Nynäs slott](https://sormlandsmuseum.se/besoksinfo/nynas-slott/)

### 1.10 Grisslehamn — archipelago village at the end of Roslagsleden (**open all winter**)
**What:** Small archipelago port at the end of väg 76/Roslagsleden: harbour, coastal path along the
shore, motionsspår; Albert Engström's studio/gården (seasonal museum).

**Drive:** ~65 min, ~75 km via väg 76.

**November caveats:** coastal paths open year-round; museum, shops and cafés are seasonal — verify
before relying on them. Exposed seafront: cold and windy in November.

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=60.102200,18.801200) · [geo:](geo:60.102200,18.801200) — Grisslehamn harbour

**Source:** [norrtalje.se](https://www.norrtalje.se/), [sv.wikipedia.org — Grisslehamn](https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grisslehamn)

### 1.11 Femöre — coastal cliffs and a closed Cold War fort (**open all winter**; fort closed)
**What:** 235 ha peninsula reserve south of Oxelösund: cliffs, sea-buckthorn, coastal pine forest;
Sörmlandsleden's "Femöre runt" loops it. Under Femörehuvud sits **Femörefortet**, an intact 1966–2003
coastal artillery battery.

**Drive:** ~85 min, ~120–125 km via E4 to Nyköping then väg 53 to Oxelösund, over Femörekanalen.

**November caveats:** reserve **open year-round** (verified); the fort's last public 2026 tours ended
30 October — **in November the fort interior is closed** (group bookings only). Winter birding is good
(sea eagles, eiders in the ice-free channel). Exposed cliffs: wind, spray ice, slippery rocks; never
trust the ice in Femörekanalen. Fire only at designated grillplatser.

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=58.653173,17.107627) · [geo:](geo:58.653173,17.107627) — Femörefortets parkering (also fiskehamn parking: [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=58.655962,17.099209) · [geo:](geo:58.655962,17.099209))

**Source:** [länsstyrelsen.se — Femöre](https://www.lansstyrelsen.se/sodermanland/besoksmal/naturreservat/femore.html), [femorefortet.se](https://www.femorefortet.se/), [visitoxelosund.se](https://visitoxelosund.se/)

## 2. Towns & castles

### 2.1 Rosersbergs slott — royal palace and park (**park open all winter**; interior closed)
**What:** One of Sweden's best-preserved royal palaces (Karl XIV Johan/Desideria-era interiors), baroque
garden and English landscape park with views over Sigtunafjärden; Ingegerdsleden starts here.

**Drive:** ~35 min, ~35 km via E4 north, exit 179 Rosersberg.

**November caveats (verified):** castle **closed for the 2026 season — guided tours June–September
only**; furnishings winter-covered Sept–May, so no drop-in; groups can pre-book. **Slottsparken open
year-round** — go midday, paths unlit. The Slottshotell restaurant/café is a separate food stop (§3.1).

**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=59.574951,17.843381) · [geo:](geo:59.574951,17.843381) — Rosersbergs slott, parking below the castle

### 2.12 Sala silvergruva — 500-year silver mine (**open daily, book ahead**)
**What:** Sala Silvergruva: guided tours 155 m down into the 500-year-old silver mine, museum,
Konstmästargården; the town has its kyrka and Gruvfolkskör choir tradition.

**Drive:** ~90 min, ~140–150 km via E18 → väg 70 (at the outer edge of the 1.5 h limit — leave early).

**November caveats (verified):** mine tours **every day** (book via the calendar — popular slots sell
out; age limit 5); Konstmästargården 10:00–16:00, museum 10:00–16:00. **Lucia weekend 11–13 Dec 2026:
