# Day trips by car from Stockholm — November 2026

Lesser-known destinations within ~2.5 hours' drive of T-Centralen, for a month with short days,
off-season opening hours and the first snow/ice. One main destination per day — no multi-stop loops.
All opening hours below were checked on official sites on **2026-11-07**; anything that could not be
verified is listed at the end. Every entry has a Google Maps link and a `geo:` link with GPS
coordinates taken from the Google Maps pin of the entrance / parking lot / POI itself (all within
~200 m of the actual spot).

## November conditions — read this first

- **Daylight:** 1 Nov sunrise ≈ 07:00, sunset ≈ 16:35; by 30 Nov ≈ 08:16 – 15:25 (monthly average
  07:44 / 15:23). Plan outdoor time roughly **09:00–15:00** and bring a headlamp.
- **Roads:** winter tyres are mandatory 1 Dec–31 Mar in winter conditions and strongly recommended
  already in November; expect black ice (svartis) on bridges, in shadows and on gravel roads
  (several trailhead roads are unploughed gravel).
- **Fire rules:** no eldningsförbud is *usually* in force in November, but one can be declared at any
  time — check the day-of status on [krisinformation.se](https://www.krisinformation.se/forbered-dig/gras--och-skogsbrand/eldningsforbud/)
  or the **Brandrisk Ute** app. In all reserves below, make fires/grill **only at the designated,
  fixed fireplaces** (iordningställda eldplatser); a camping stove (friluftskök) is generally allowed.
  Never burn on bare rock, moss or peat; extinguish completely.
- **Ice:** do **not** walk on sea or lake ice in November — SMHI warns thin ice can be weaker than
  thick ice. Wet coastal rocks and suspension bridges get slippery: bring spikes (broddar).
- **Ferries:** if you add an archipelago island, note Waxholmsbolaget runs a reduced autumn timetable
  in November and switches to the full winter timetable on **10 December 2026**
  ([waxholmsbolaget.se](https://waxholmsbolaget.se/trafikinformation/10-december-gar-vi-over-till-vintertidtabell)) — check the day's departures before driving to the jetty.

## 1. Hikes & nature

### 1.1 Stendörren nature reserve — walk to the archipelago on suspension bridges
**What:** 923 ha archipelago reserve (Natura 2000) on the Sörmland coast between Nyköping and Trosa.
Four suspension bridges take you from the mainland out to the islands Stora Krokholmen and Äspskär;
3 marked trails (incl. the 9 km Stora Stendörrenrundan), bathing rocks, an observation tower and many
fixed fireplaces with firewood. **Drive:** ~1 h 10 min, ~100 km via E4 south, then väg 219 toward
Studsvik/"Stendörren" (last stretch is a gravel road). **November caveats:** the reserve itself is
**always open** (verified, länsstyrelsen.se), but **Naturum Stendörren is closed for the season until
1 May 2027** — no exhibitions, shop, guided activities or café (verified on the official page), so
bring your own fika. The suspension bridges can be slippery/icy and windy — check status before you
go. Fire only at the designated fireplaces. Two parkings: Entré Äspskär (first lot) and Entré
Krokholmen (~500 m further in).
**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=58.748931,17.380632) · [geo:](geo:58.748931,17.380632) — Entré Äspskär parking (Krokholmen parking: [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=58.746666,17.387410) · [geo:](geo:58.746666,17.387410))
*Source: lansstyrelsen.se/sodermanland/besoksmal/naturreservat/stendorren.html, .../naturum-stendorren.html*

### 1.2 Billudden — the esker that dives into the sea
**What:** 1,904 ha reserve where Uppsalaåsen (the esker) ends in Gävlebukten, ~10 km north of
Älvkarleby. Klapper beaches, sea-buckthorn thickets, the old fishing hamlet Billhamn, Rullsand beach
and an 11+ km trail network; a 2.5 km nature trail starts at the Billhamn parking. **Drive:**
~1 h 30–45 min, ~150–160 km via E4 north, then väg 76 and the signed gravel road past Rullsand
toward Långsand/"Billudden". **November caveats:** open year-round and free to park in winter
(verified via länsstyrelsen.se/uppsala and naturkartan.se), but the small naturstuga (Billuddens
naturum) is open June–August only → **closed in November**. Fully exposed peninsula: wind, cold and
icy rocks; fire only at the designated fireplaces; no tenting outside the camping area.
**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=60.646548,17.501971) · [geo:](geo:60.646548,17.501971) — "Billudden parkering" by Billhamn
*Source: lansstyrelsen.se/uppsala/besoksmal/naturreservat/billudden.html, billhamn.se*

### 1.3 Tyresta national park — old-growth forest and forest lakes
**What:** Stockholm's nearest national park (~5,000 ha with the adjoining reserve), 55 km of trails,
one of Sweden's finest old-growth forests. The 2.5 km **Urskogsstigen** starts at the main entrance
Tyresta by, next to Naturum Nationalparkernas hus (free entry) and Tyresta gård. **Drive:** ~35–45 min,
~30 km via väg 73 and väg 260 toward Brandbergen. **November caveats:** the park is **open 24/7
year-round** (verified, sverigesnationalparker.se). Naturum's published hours cover Jan–Mar
(Wed–Fri 10–15) and Apr–Oct (Tue–Sun 10–16) but **do not state November explicitly** — Haninge kommun
says "Tue–Sun all year", which conflicts; treat naturum hours as **unverified for November** and check
the page before going. Parking at Tyresta by is signposted and fee-based; forest roads are not ploughed
in winter. Fire only at the designated eldrastplatser (firewood sold by Swish).
**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=59.168945,18.236530) · [geo:](geo:59.168945,18.236530) — Naturum / huvudentré Tyresta by
*Source: sverigesnationalparker.se (Tyresta, Naturum Nationalparkernas hus)*

### 1.4 Femöre peninsula — coastal cliffs, and a closed Cold War fort
**What:** 235 ha reserve on the peninsula south of Oxelösund: cliffs, sea-buckthorn, coastal pine
forest; the Sörmlandsleden "Femöre runt" loop goes around it. Under Femörehuvud sits **Femörefortet**,
an intact 1966–2003 coastal artillery battery blasted into the rock. **Drive:** ~1 h 20–30 min,
~120–125 km via E4 to Nyköping then väg 53 to Oxelösund, over Femörekanalen. **November caveats:**
the reserve is **open year-round** (verified, länsstyrelsen.se/sodermanland), but the fort's last public
2026 tours ended 30 October — **in November the fort interior is closed** (group bookings only)
(verified, femorefortet.se / visitoxelosund.se). Winter birding is good (sea eagles, eiders in the
ice-free channel). Exposed cliffs: wind, spray ice, slippery rocks; never trust the ice in
Femörekanalen. Fire only at designated grillplatser.
**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=58.653173,17.107627) · [geo:](geo:58.653173,17.107627) — Femörefortets parkering (stora parkeringen; also fiskehamn parking: [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=58.655962,17.099209) · [geo:](geo:58.655962,17.099209))
*Source: lansstyrelsen.se/sodermanland/besoksmal/naturreservat/femore.html, femorefortet.se*

### 1.5 Nynäs — manor park and coastal trails (castle closed for the season)
**What:** Nynäs slott by Rundbosjön surrounded by the 3,830 ha Nynäs naturreservat — pastures, hollow
oaks, forest and coastline; Sörmlandsleden stages 51–52 meet at the castle. STF's vandrarhem
"Bränneriet" is on site. **Drive:** ~55–65 min, ~75–90 km via E4, Tystberga exit signed "Nynäs slott"
(or coastal väg 219). **November caveats (verified, nynasslott.se/besoksinformation/):** the
**reserve, park grounds and trails are open year-round**, but the **castle, café, exhibitions and shop
are closed until summer 2027** — bring food and water, no services on site; trails can be muddy; dark
from ~15:45.
**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=58.807945,17.366226) · [geo:](geo:58.807945,17.366226) — Nynäs slott and its signed parking
*Source: nynasslott.se/besoksinformation/, sormlandsmuseum.se/besoksinfo/nynas-slott/*

### 1.6 Bogesundslandet — peninsula hiking and a castle ruin
**What:** 28.9 km² peninsula (half of Vaxholm kommun), a reserve since 2015, with marked
walking/riding/cycling trails, bathing at Fridhemsbadet, and **Bogesunds slott** (1640s, Per Brahe d.y.)
in the middle; STF Bogesund slottsvandrarhem is next door. **Drive:** ~45–55 min, ~40 km via väg 274
over the Vaxholm bridge, then Pålsundsvägen and Per Brahes väg to the castle parking. **November
caveats:** the reserve is **open year-round** (verified, länsstyrelsen.se/stockholm), but there are **no
public castle tours in November** — summer tours ran 6/6–13/9 2026 and the Christmas-market tours are
12–13 Dec 2026 (verified, bogesundsslott.se); the castle is a private residence, grounds open. Rocky
shores icy — spikes; wind off Stora Värtan.
**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=59.394016,18.286209) · [geo:](geo:59.394016,18.286209) — Bogesunds slott / Per Brahes väg parking
*Source: lansstyrelsen.se/stockholm/besoksmal/naturreservat/bogesundslandet.html, bogesundsslott.se*

## 2. Towns & castles

### 2.1 Trosa — wooden town on the Trosaån, with an Advent Christmas market
**What:** Picture-book small town at the mouth of Trosaån: Trosa Torg, Västra/Östra Långgatan, the old
harbour and canal, medieval Trosa kyrka, and Garvaregården open-air museum next to the square.
**Drive:** ~1 h 10 min, ~74 km via E4 south + väg 219. **November caveats:** the town is always
accessible, but it is off-season — several summer restaurants close after September (e.g. Trosa
Sjökrog) and others reduce to Fri–Sun (see §3.2 Kölsvinet). Verified event: **Trosa julmarknad,
Sunday 29 November 2026 (1st Advent), 10:00–15:00** at Trosa Torg/Garvaregården (trosa.com). Tourist
office winter hours not verified.
**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=58.895934,17.547115) · [geo:](geo:58.895934,17.547115) — Trosa Torg
*Source: trosa.com (julmarknad 29 nov 2026), ruttplanerare.se (74 km/1 h 10 min)*

### 2.2 Nyköping — Nyköpingshus castle and Sörmlands museum
**What:** Sörmland's county seat on the Nyköpingsån. **Nyköpingshus** (origins 1100s–1200s; the 1317
Nyköpings gästabud) with the white Kungstornet tower hosting three exhibitions; **Sörmlands museum**
(Tolagsgatan 8, opposite the castle) has the main exhibitions, café and shop; stroll Åpromenaden and
Norra Berget. **Drive:** ~1 h 10–20 min, ~90–102 km via E4 south. **November caveats (verified,
sormlandsmuseum.se):** Sörmlands museum **Tue–Sun 10:00–17:00, closed Mondays** (Kulturkväll open to
21:00 on Wed 18 Nov 2026); **Kungstornet Sundays 10:00–16:00** in the low season; **julmarknad with
FREE entry to both sites Sat 28 – Sun 29 Nov 2026**. Ticket 80 kr (free ≤18) covers both sites one day.
Castle ruins/tower are outdoors and unheated — dress warm. Regular guided-tour times in November not
verified.
**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=58.748889,17.011111) · [geo:](geo:58.748889,17.011111) — Nyköpingshus (museum parking on Tolagsgatan opposite)
*Source: sormlandsmuseum.se/besoksinfo/sormlands-museum/ and /besoksinfo/nykopingshus/*

### 2.3 Söderköping — Göta kanal locks and a ghost of an ice-cream summer
**What:** Medieval town where Göta kanal meets the Baltic: Storgatan's low wooden houses, the
hand-operated canal locks, Rådhustorget and Ramunderberget with the view over town and archipelago.
**Drive:** ~1 h 20–30 min, ~115–120 km via E4 south, Söderköping exit. **November caveats:** the
town is open, but the famous **Söderköpings Glass / Smultronstället is closed for the season (2026
season ended 30 Aug — verified, smultronstallet.se)** and the camping café closes 8 Nov 2026. Locks are
not operating in winter. Year-round lunch options exist (Söderköpings Brunn, Österns Stjärna, Stockrosen
Café) but their exact November hours were **not verified**. The adventsmarknad is the first December
weekend — not in November.
**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=58.481977,16.320255) · [geo:](geo:58.481977,16.320255) — Rådhustorget (canal locks: [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=58.488693,16.295662) · [geo:](geo:58.488693,16.295662))
*Source: smultronstallet.se/oppettider/, soderkopingscamping.se, soderkoping.se*

### 2.4 Strömsholms slott — palace island, closed interior, open park
**What:** Royal palace built by Gustav Vasa in the 1550s on a holm in Kolbäcksån (a natural moat),
~20 km SW of Västerås; Gustavian interiors, Hårleman's chapel, the historic riding school; the
surrounding grounds are a nature reserve down to Lake Mälaren. **Drive:** ~1 h 20–45 min, ~115–130 km
via E18 west. **November caveats (verified, royalpalaces.se / kungligaslotten.se):** the castle
**"stängt för säsongen 2026" — no public tours in November** (summer-only; groups can pre-book
year-round). The **park/grounds are walkable** (free, outdoor; formal winter opening not verified —
SFV's page was down when checked). A rumoured "Jul på Strömsholm" Christmas market was **not found for
2026 — do not plan around it**; nearest confirmed market in the municipality is Westerqwarn, Kolbäck,
31 Oct–1 Nov 2026 (already past in late November). Slottscafé "Stenköket" is seasonal; November status
not verified. Dark from ~15:50; park paths unlit.
**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=59.524803,16.269816) · [geo:](geo:59.524803,16.269816) — Strömsholms slott
*Source: royalpalaces.se/vara-besoksmal/stromsholms-slott/oppettider.html, kungligaslotten.se*

### 2.5 Rosersbergs slott — Karl XIV Johan interiors (summer only) and a year-round park
**What:** One of Sweden's best-preserved royal palaces (interiors nearly untouched from the
Karl XIV Johan/Desideria era), with a baroque garden and English landscape park with views over
Sigtunafjärden; the Ingegerdsleden trail starts here. Rosersbergs Slottshotell (hotel/restaurant/café)
operates year-round. **Drive:** ~35 min, ~35 km via E4 north, exit 179 Rosersberg. **November caveats
(verified, kungligaslotten.se / rosersbergsslott.se):** the castle is **closed for the 2026 season —
guided tours run June–September only**, and September–May the furnishings are winter-covered with no
modern lighting, so no drop-in visits; groups can pre-book. The **slottspark is open year-round** — go
midday, paths unlit and sunset ~16:00 in November. The Slottshotell's café/restaurant operates
year-round (exact November hours not verified).
**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=59.574951,17.843381) · [geo:](geo:59.574951,17.843381) — Rosersbergs slott, parking below the castle
*Source: kungligaslotten.se/vara-besoksmal/rosersbergs-slott/oppettider.html, rosersbergsslott.se*

### 2.6 Steninge slott & slottsby — manor park, shops and two Advent market weekends
**What:** Late-17th-century manor (Nicodemus Tessin the Elder) by Lake Mälaren, ~1.5 km from Märsta.
The castle is a private residence (interior closed year-round), but the **slottspark is open to the
public daily 12:00–16:30**, and around it Steninge slottsby: Stenladan shops, Trälogen and
**Restaurang Steninge Bruk** (see §3.5). **Drive:** ~40–45 min, ~45 km via E4 north, exit 180, väg 263
toward Märsta/Steninge. **November caveats (verified window on steningeslottsby.se; market dates from
consistent listings on julmarknad.nu et al.):** **"Jul i Stenladan" runs daily 8 Nov – 23 Dec 2026**;
**julmarknad in Trälogen Sat 28 – Sun 29 Nov and 5–6 Dec 2026, 11:00–16:00** (arrive early for parking).
Stenladan shops otherwise Mon–Fri 11:00–18:00, Sat–Sun 10:00–17:00. Park closes 16:30 — go midday.
**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=59.603754,17.802121) · [geo:](geo:59.603754,17.802121) — Steninge slott, Steninge Slottsväg 141
*Source: steningeslottsby.se, steningebruk.se, julmarknad.nu/event/julmarknad-steninge-slottsby/*

## 3. Restaurants & cafés (food-first day trips)

### 3.1 Taxinge-Näsby slott ("Kakslottet") — cake buffet in a castle, weekends only
**What:** 17th–19th c. castle on a peninsula in Gripsholmsviken, run as a café with "the Nordic region's
largest cake buffet" (~65 homemade cakes/breads, pay per item) plus smörgåsar and light dishes, served
in the castle library or park. Slottslunch/menus are for pre-booked groups ≥15. **Drive:** ~50–60 min,
~58 km via E4/E20 south to the Nykvarn exit. **November caveats (verified, taxingeslott.se/oppettider/):
the Slottscafé is open Fri–Sun 11:00–16:00 October–April — i.e. closed Mon–Thu in November — and
closed Christmas/New Year. No November afternoon-tea dates were published (2026 season ended in
October). Jul på Taxinge-Näsby Christmas market 13–15 and 19–22 Nov 2026 (entry fee those days, even
for coffee only). Plan a Fri–Sun visit.**
**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=59.246944,17.309167) · [geo:](geo:59.246944,17.309167) — Taxinge-Näsby slott, Nykvarn
*Source: taxingeslott.se/oppettider/, /slottscafe/, /afternoon-tea/*

### 3.2 Kölsvinet, Trosa guest harbour — wood-fired pizza by the quay, Fri–Sun only
**What:** Harbour-front restaurant/bar in Trosa gästhamn: wood-oven Neapolitan pizza, fish/seafood,
terrace on the pontoons; lunch, dinner, events. **Drive:** ~55 min–1 h 10 min to Trosa (~65–74 km,
E4 + väg 219). **November caveats (verified, kolsvinet.se + trosa.com "open all year"):** **Fri–Sat
11:00–21:00, Sun 11:00–15:00, closed Mon–Thu** — no seasonal note published, so these hours apply in
November 2026; go Fri–Sun. Pair with a stroll in Trosa (§2.1; julmarknad 29 Nov). Note Trosa Sjökrog
is **closed for the season** after September.
**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=58.890812,17.552446) · [geo:](geo:58.890812,17.552446) — Kölsvinet, Uddbergagatan, Trosa gästhamn
*Source: kolsvinet.se, trosa.com/en/eat-drink/open-all-year/, trosasjokrog.se/oppettider/*

### 3.3 Kökeriet (Nynäs Rökeri) — smoked fish lunch in Nynäshamn harbour
**What:** Harbour restaurant of the Nynäs Rökeri smokehouse, right on the fish quay in Nynäshamn:
fish soup, baked haddock, hot-smoked salmon, räkmacka, weekday lunch; the fish shop next door sells
smoked goods by weight. **Drive:** ~50 min, ~55–60 km via väg 73. **November caveats (verified,
nynasrokeri.se/oppetider, November section):** Kökeriet **closed Mondays; Tue–Fri 11:30–15:00,
Sat 11:30–16:00, Sun 11:30–15:00** — lunch-only in November, so arrive early afternoon; Saturday is
best. (Some Fridays are booked for private "räkfrossa".) The fish shop: Mon–Thu 10–18, Fri 9:30–18,
Sat 10–16, Sun closed. Note **Nynäs slott's own café is closed until summer 2027** — pair with a walk
in the Nynäs reserve (§1.5) if you like.
**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=58.900869,17.952397) · [geo:](geo:58.900869,17.952397) — Kökeriet, Fiskargränd 6, Nynäshamn
*Source: nynasrokeri.se/oppetider, nynasrokeri.se/kokeriet/*

### 3.4 Julita gård — bistro & café in a museum estate (winter weekends only)
**What:** Nordiska museet's preserved early-1900s estate by lake Öljaren, ~25 km NW of Katrineholm:
manor, park, cultural-historical gardens and a bistro/café (Trädgårdscaféet). **Drive:** ~1 h 30–45 min,
~165 km via E4/E20. **November caveats (verified, julitagard.se/besok-oss/oppettider/):** in autumn/
winter the buildings, shop and museum are closed, the **park is open daily for self-guided visits
(free)**, and **bistro & café are open Fri–Sun 10:00–16:00 during 2 Oct – 20 Dec** — so in November
**food is available only Fri–Sun 10:00–16:00; Mon–Thu = park walk only**. Dark by ~15:30–16:00 — go
midday. The 2026 julmarknad (23–25 Oct) is past.
**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=59.149022,16.046001) · [geo:](geo:59.149022,16.046001) — Julita gård entrance/parking
*Source: julitagard.se/besok-oss/oppettider/*

### 3.5 Restaurang Steninge Bruk — lunch and weekend brunch in the old stone barn
**What:** Restaurant in the stone barn at Steninge slottsby (Märsta): open-fire grill, lunch,
weekend brunch, café. **Drive:** ~40–45 min, ~45 km via E4 north, exit 180 + väg 263. **November caveats
(standard hours published on steningebruk.se; no November deviations found — treat as verified pattern,
not a November-specific guarantee):** lunch Mon–Fri 11:00–14:30; **brunch/helglunch Sat–Sun 10:30–16:00**;
café Mon–Fri 11:00–18:00 (Fri to 22:00), Sat–Sun 10:00–17:00. Combine with the slottspark (§2.6) — park
closes 16:30 — and the julmarknad weekends 28–29 Nov.
**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=59.605194,17.800427) · [geo:](geo:59.605194,17.800427) — Restaurang Steninge Bruk, Steninge Slottsväg 141, Märsta
*Source: steningebruk.se, steningeslottsby.se*

## The standards you've already done (one-liners for November)

- **Mariefred** (~1 h, E4): Gripsholms slott — check reduced winter tour hours on kungligaslotten.se
  before going; the town and Gripsholm's malmgård area are fine for a winter walk.
- **Sigtuna** (~40 min, E4 norr): medieval town centre; most cafés/restaurants open year-round but with
  reduced winter hours — check individually.
- **Uppsala** (~1 h, E4 norr): cathedral and university; museums keep normal winter hours (check each).
- **Drottningholm** (~30 min, väg 275/178): palace park open year-round; the palace and Drottningholms
  slottsteater have off-season hours — closed or guided-only in November (kungligaslotten.se).
- **Waxholm** (ferry from Strömkajen or car via väg 274): Vaxholms fästning museum has reduced winter
  hours; **Waxholmsbolaget switches to the winter timetable on 10 Dec 2026** and already runs reduced
  service in November — check departures at waxholmsbolaget.se before you go.
